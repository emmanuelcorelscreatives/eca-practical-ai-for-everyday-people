# Source scope worksheet
Target: plan a weekday workshop at Bellwether Central Library on 17 November 2026. Check closing time, local booking rules, current program lead and setup for NoteMaster 5 on Android, Community edition.

1. Open `index.html`. Read the boxed fictional request and deliberately flawed specimen summary. Copy each claim exactly into `source-checking-worksheet.csv` or the editable browser worksheet.
2. Before inspecting evidence, write required time, place, subject/role and software scope. Do not silently replace the claim with an easier one.
3. Open each linked source. Use your browser's Find in Page for `8:00`, `Applies only`, `Samira` and `NoteMaster`. Record the passage, its source file, dates and limitations. Search previews are leads; the opened page is the exercise evidence.
4. For hours, compare publication, effective period, event and update dates. Does a © 2026 footer renew a 2024 schedule? Open the current calendar and search for `closing`; distinguish absence of information from proof of a closing time.
5. For booking, compare North District with Bellwether City. Then use the branch venue page to write only the narrower supported rule. Do not import fees, deadlines or permissions from the other jurisdiction.
6. For the person, check name, organization, role and period together. Write a safe replacement if the cited role does not establish the current library lead.
7. For software, record the About specimen's version, platform and edition. Compare both documentation pages and quote the setup passage and limitation that actually match.
8. Record supported, mismatched or unresolved and a correction. A mismatch is not proof the original claim is false. The final booking status may be “supported after narrowing” but preserve the original mismatched claim in your notes.
9. Compare with `answers.md`. Finish by explaining why historical evidence can answer a historical question while failing a current one.

Expected checks: all four rows have required scope, opened passage and source, source scope, outcome and correction; closing time stays unknown; no current named lead is invented; the old product's File menu does not become Android guidance. Browser download saves your work locally; nothing is sent externally.

Manual fallback without the workbook: read `sources/*.html`, complete the CSV in a spreadsheet or copy the five worksheet headings onto paper. For a real task, use ordinary search with exact claim plus responsible organization, open the actual result, find the passage and read qualifications. Record access date; recheck changeable details near use. Archive captures can be missing or incomplete. Consequential decisions require appropriate authoritative context and qualified review.
