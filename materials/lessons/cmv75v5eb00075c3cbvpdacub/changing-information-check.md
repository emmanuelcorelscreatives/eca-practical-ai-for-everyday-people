# Check a changing fact
Exercise question: “When does the garden open tomorrow?”

This is a fictional garden: there is no official live listing supplied. The correct outcome from this kit alone is UNRESOLVED. Do not search for an unrelated real garden and treat it as this fictional event.

For a real event, record:
- Exact place and date being asked about.
- Responsible organizer's current listing URL and access date/time.
- Exact source wording and its date/place coverage.
- Whether the opening-hours field is actually populated.
- Any conflict with an older repost or another location.
- What remains unresolved and who to contact.

Search results are a route to a source, not proof. Open the page. A link with the wrong date/place, an old repost, a blank field or an assistant's summary alone cannot establish the answer. Compare each summary claim directly with the source passage.

For health, safety, legal rights or money, use current authoritative evidence and appropriately qualified judgment. This worksheet provides no advice in those domains.
