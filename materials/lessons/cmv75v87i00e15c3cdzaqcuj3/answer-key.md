# Answer key — open after your own attempt
## Fresh case
30% means 30 per 100. 30 / 100 = 0.30. Apply this to 50: 0.30 × 50 = 15.
Independent check: 10% of 50 = 5; three groups of 10% give 30%, so 3 × 5 = 15.

## Mapping expectations
Matches: equal parts, selected part compared with the whole, and per-hundred scale.
Stops matching: real quantities need not be 100 objects; continuous quantities and decimals are possible; percentages can exceed 100 when the reference comparison permits it.

## Decision rule
Keep a comparison if it clarifies the important relationship. Revise or discard it if the differences imply a false conclusion. A worked example must show its problem, reasoning, intermediate steps and an independent check. Finally, apply the actual concept to a fresh quantity without relying on the analogy.
