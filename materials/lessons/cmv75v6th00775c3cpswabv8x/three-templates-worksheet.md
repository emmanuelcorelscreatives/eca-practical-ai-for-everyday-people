# Add a check to three templates
Use only self-created or permission-cleared inputs. A generated answer is a draft, not independent evidence.

For each task fill in: **Input / Instruction / Output format / Quality check / Evidence / Accept–Revise–Stop rule / Destination and approval**.

## 1. Announcement
Use `source-notes.txt`. Specify the readers and destination. Write a check for exact names, dates, time, place and conditions; unsupported claims; missing registration; image permission; readable output. Apply it to both drafts in `worked-announcements.md`. Record one finding beside each criterion.

## 2. Expense total
Fictional stationery expenses: notebooks, 3 at 4 currency units each; pens, 2 at 1.50 currency units each. No tax, delivery or discount is specified. Ask for an itemized total; check source quantities and units, then independently recalculate in a calculator or spreadsheet. Do not invent tax or a currency country. Record the calculation and compare to the answer.

## 3. Source summary
Self-authored source: “The reading room opens at 10:00 on Saturday. Visitors may enter without booking, except that groups of more than six must arrange a time beforehand.”
Ask for a one-sentence summary. Check the opening time/day and preserve the exception; prohibit invented prices or access promises. Compare against the actual original, not an assistant's second opinion.

## Review each template
What could be wrong? How would I detect it? What will I do if the check fails? Can another learner apply this check without guessing what ‘good’ means?

Stop before any consequential external action. Stronger review and capable human help may be needed for meaning, translation, permissions or consequences beyond this practice.
