# Lesson 177 — Repurpose Approved Content
Emmanuel Corels Academy • Stage 30

This self-created fictional practice pack supports the lesson. No event, person, registration or publication is real. The source remains the authority; generated or authored sample drafts are not owner approval.

## Complete exercise
1. Open `approved-source.md`; save an untouched copy in `01 Approved Source`.
2. Read `prompt.txt`. Paste the complete source below the SOURCE boundary into any available assistant, or use the manual route below. Do not connect sending or publishing tools.
3. Save three labelled outputs in `02 Generated Drafts`: email, social, poster copy. Compare them with the source, never with one another.
4. Open `verification-template.csv` in a spreadsheet (or a text editor). Record the actual wording/evidence for every channel and every fact. A check means you compared meaning, not that wording sounded plausible. Missing source details remain unspecified.
5. Compare `faulty-drafts.md` and record each defect before reading `answer-key.md`. Correct only the draft; do not silently change the approved source.
6. Copy your poster text manually into a design tool or ordinary document. Use large readable headings, strong contrast, margins and no decorative element that obscures facts. Inspect the actual export at phone width and at full size for clipping, spelling and numbers. The supplied `poster.svg` is an editable self-authored example; it is not a screenshot or a published event poster.
7. Save checked versions in `03 Checked Final` and older attempts in `04 Archive`. Complete `owner-review-template.md`. Leave external publication status NOT SENT until a responsible human explicitly authorizes destination/timing/content. This exercise never sends anything.

## Manual fallback
Without an assistant, copy the six factual source lines into three new documents. Add a subject and greeting to the email, remove repetitions for social, and arrange the six facts in a poster. Use the same comparison matrix and human review. Without a spreadsheet use a four-column table on screen. Without a design tool use a document editor and print/export preview.

## Success criteria
All six supplied facts appear without changed promises in each checked channel. No new deadline, registration rule, contact, capacity, eligibility exclusion or repair guarantee appears. Missing details are recorded as unspecified; any required missing information blocks external approval and is sent to the fictional owner as a question. The poster is readable and unclipped. All outputs remain review copies. The owner review record and correction log remain honest (blank until performed).

## Deliverables
Untouched approved source; three labelled drafts; checked versions; completed verification matrix; at least five corrected defects with reasons; poster export; unsigned owner-review record. `expected-verification.csv` and `answer-key.md` are teaching references, not claims you completed the exercise.
