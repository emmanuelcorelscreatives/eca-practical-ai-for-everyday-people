Work with the SOURCE DOCUMENT and the deliberately flawed AUTHORED EXAMPLE side by side. Allow about 25 minutes; work at your own pace. Read answers.md only after your first attempt. Optional assistant use is described in prompts.md.

1. Copy the four profiles into evidence-table.csv. Keep preference, experience, availability and access requirements separate. Write Unknown for unrecorded facts. Do not turn “none stated” into “none needed.”
2. For each draft row, underline the unsupported assumption, identify a conflicting source fact or missing fact, and record who loses choice or opportunity or receives a burden. Use review-record.csv. Find at least four distinct unsupported claims.
3. Apply these five questions to each recommendation:
   - What does the source actually say?
   - What has the assistant or draft assumed?
   - Has a group label replaced individual evidence?
   - Whose needs or perspectives are missing?
   - Would changing an irrelevant identity detail change the recommendation?
   Then check the consequences: who gains, loses, carries burdens or disappears? Examine roles and resources as well as wording.

With an assistant, run the same request with each input, save the exact actual responses, and compare assignment, rationale, unknowns and access support. Record differences or no difference honestly. Do not invent a changed result. Explain why either outcome cannot certify fairness.
