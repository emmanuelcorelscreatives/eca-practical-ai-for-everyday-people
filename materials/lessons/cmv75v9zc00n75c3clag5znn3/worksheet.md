# Lesson 141 — Prepare a Tailored Application Draft
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Self-created fictional practice kit

No real applicant, employer, credential or application is represented. Nothing in this kit is submitted. The supplied listing and applicant evidence are opened practice sources, not generated proof.

## Practice workflow
1. Read job-listing.md. Separate required from desirable points and preserve unknown details.
2. Open applicant-evidence.csv. Identify exactly which actions are supported; do not infer team management, qualifications or outcomes.
3. Use bounded-prompt.txt with an available assistant, or draft manually using application-template.md. Keep any generated response labelled “Generated draft — review required.” An assistant response is not evidence.
4. Complete claim-review.csv for every meaningful claim in your own draft. Remove unsupported claims and replace vague praise with supported actions.
5. Compare against worked-example.md only after your attempt. Wording can differ; facts cannot.
6. Apply final-checklist.md. Save locally. Do not submit the fictional example.

## Learning check
A successful draft names Community Programs Coordinator, connects scheduling four volunteer sessions to coordination and weekly plain-language updates to communication, makes no attendee/outcome/qualification/staff-supervision claims, and leaves unresolved information visibly unresolved. Lack of evidence for a requirement remains visible, rather than becoming an invented credential.

## Manual fallback
Use a text or document editor: copy each relevant requirement, write a supported matching action, then check it against the evidence sheet. No paid product or special assistant feature is required. Check your actual account access; interfaces and permissions vary.

## Files
job-listing.md is the opened fictional listing. applicant-evidence.csv is the practice source boundary. bounded-prompt.txt is a reusable instruction. application-template.md and claim-review.csv are exercises. worked-example.md and final-checklist.md provide expected checks. All names and facts are self-created for this lesson; replace them with permission-cleared personal information only in your private copy.
