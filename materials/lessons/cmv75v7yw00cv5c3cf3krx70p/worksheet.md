# Summarize a Long Message Thread — Lesson 79

Use the fictional Riverside Room Booking thread to distinguish confirmed decisions, unresolved questions and requested actions. You need a text editor; AI is optional. Nothing in this kit sends messages.

1. Open `thread.txt`. Count six message boundaries. Read each current body before its quoted history; identify sender and date. A repeated quotation does not renew a proposal.
2. List the confirmed date and start time with supporting message. Explain why Tuesday is absent from current decisions.
3. List unanswered questions. Distinguish room access from the newest specific key-collector question; do not invent an access arrangement.
4. Record the projector request, deadline, missing owner and missing answer. Explain why being a recipient or confirming a booking does not assign a task.
5. Optionally use `prompt.txt` with the complete thread in a permitted assistant. Label your result “Generated draft — not verified.” Check names, confidentiality, attachments, tool access and permission before using any real source. Record the actual tool and your own observed output; no tool is required.
6. Compare every draft line with original messages. Use `manual-table.csv` as a worked fallback, then make a fresh three-column table: source wording / classification / checked summary. Add missing-owner/deadline notes.
7. Repair this deliberately flawed study line: “Amina will collect the key on Tuesday.” Identify both unsupported assignment and obsolete proposal.
8. Open `thread-practice.html` offline. Expand all six messages and their history. Choose the three classification answers, check feedback, and paste your checked summary in the draft box. Verify To and Cc remain empty and status says Not sent. Save your notes yourself in a text file; the page does not persist them.
9. Compare with `answers.md` and `checked-summary.txt` only after attempting the task. This is a self-authored teaching key, not a model result.

Before using a real draft, check To, Cc, subject, attachments, all commitments and missing details yourself. Drafting does not determine recipients or authorize sending. This exercise ends with an unsent draft.
