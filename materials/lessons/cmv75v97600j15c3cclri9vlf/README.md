# Choose Decision Criteria — Academy practice kit

Emmanuel Corels Academy • Practical AI for Everyday People • Stage 20 • Lesson 116.

This self-authored, fictional community-workshop exercise teaches how to define comparable criteria **before** collecting option results or ranking anything. Harbor Room, Cedar Hall and Junction Studio are invented names, not venue recommendations. No quotation, route result, availability, accessibility certification or cancellation policy has been supplied or observed.

Open `decision-criteria-worksheet.html` locally in a browser. It works offline, makes no network requests and requires no login, AI account, installation or payment. Its tabs show authored teaching states, not a recording of an external AI conversation. The suggestions tab is explicitly a scripted course example. It does not call AI or claim a live response. Use the editable Practice tab to write your own draft; use Print or save a local copy. Edits survive switching teaching tabs in this open page, but are lost on reload or closing. Copy or print before either; no private draft is stored persistently. No scores, weights, ranking or automatic winner are produced.

Files:
- `worksheet.md`: step-by-step exercise and manual fallback.
- `criteria-template.csv`: blank importable criteria definition table.
- `criteria-worked-example.csv`: six source-matching definitions, not venue assessments.
- `evidence-preview.csv`: all option results remain Unknown.
- `decision-data.json`: fictional scenario, unspecified boundaries and constraints.
- `expected-checks.md`: acceptance checks and worked reasoning.
- `review-prompt.txt`: exact limited critique prompt.
- `decision-criteria-worksheet.html`: real local teaching application, version 1.0.

CSV import: use UTF-8 and comma delimiter. These files contain no formulas or macros. You may instead open `worksheet.md` in a basic text editor; no spreadsheet is required. A modern browser is sufficient for the local page; the lesson's recorded environment and your local environment may differ. Do not enter private attendee data or upload confidential quotations to AI.
