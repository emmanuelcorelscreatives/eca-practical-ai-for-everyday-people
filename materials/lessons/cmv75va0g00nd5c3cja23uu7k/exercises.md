# Exercises
1. Mark the two supplied role requirements. Explain why a requirement is not proof of your experience.
2. Answer the first question in your own words before reading feedback.
3. For the fictional delayed-order answer, separate what it states from assumptions about responsibility, exact outcome and personal background.
4. Reorganise that answer into Situation, Task, Action, Result. Leave missing details explicit.
5. Compare first and revised versions: does it answer the question, name your action, match the requirement and avoid unsupported achievements?
6. Use all three questions in the practice log. If no evidence exists, leave the answer blank or mark it missing rather than fabricating a success.
7. Practise aloud or read back once. Note excessive background, unclear contribution and missing result/learning point. Recording is optional and requires appropriate permission.
8. An assistant adds “increased satisfaction by 40%” or predicts you will be hired. Remove the unsupported assertion; the source supplies neither a statistic nor hiring evidence.
