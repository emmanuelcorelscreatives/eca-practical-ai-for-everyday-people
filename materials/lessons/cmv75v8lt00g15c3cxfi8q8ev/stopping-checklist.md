# Define enough evidence before collecting it
- A forecast must match the place and dates.
- A restriction must be current and checked at its direct source.
- Plant guidance must match crop, growth stage and conditions.
- Equipment information must cover the exact model.
- Missing information and unresolved gaps stay visible.

Enough means suitable evidence for a bounded answer, not every page online. No criterion is met by this planning exercise alone. All evidence remains Not searched.

Final check: every subquestion helps the original question; every row has a suitable source type; search terms preserve relevant scope; sufficiency is explicit; gaps remain visible. Fix any failure before gathering more material.
