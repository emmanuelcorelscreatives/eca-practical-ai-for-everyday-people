Draft a message — clear request without unauthorized promises

WHEN TO USE
Prepare a message for human review, never send it.

REQUIRED INPUTS
[RECIPIENT RELATIONSHIP], [PURPOSE], [FACTS], [REQUESTED RESPONSE], [DEADLINE], [TONE]

REUSABLE INSTRUCTION
Draft using only supplied facts, purpose and tone. Make the requested action clear. Do not make unauthorized promises or assume attachments, permissions, names or deadlines. Flag missing information and conflicting commitments. Return a draft only; do not send.

FICTIONAL SAMPLE INPUT
RELATIONSHIP: volunteer coordinator. PURPOSE: ask for the tool collection location. FACTS: I plan to attend the community garden session at 09:00 on Saturday 24 October 2026; the meeting point is the east gate. RESPONSE: confirm tool collection location. DEADLINE: not supplied. TONE: polite and concise.

GENERATED SAMPLE OUTPUT — AI-generated teaching draft, not evidence of an external action or measured assistant trial
Hello, I plan to attend the community garden session at 09:00 on Saturday 24 October 2026, meeting at the east gate. Could you please confirm where volunteers should collect tools? Thank you.
Review note: no response deadline was supplied; no attachment is claimed.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKS
Recipient relationship and requested action are correct; date/time/location match; no deadline, attachment or promise is invented. Check before sending.
