# Planned 11-minute lesson map
These are authored-timeline teaching allocations, not observed speaking or recording measurements. The message itself targets about 45 seconds; this is distinct from the 11-minute lesson.

- 00:00–01:36: One purpose, audience, requested response, source permissions and timing estimate.
- 01:36–02:49: Fictional source notes, instruction and source/generated-output separation.
- 02:49–04:29: Inspect the draft, compare facts, delete unsupported wording and preserve one action.
- 04:29–06:28: Natural read-through, pauses, scope reduction and familiar language.
- 06:28–08:06: Check facts, unresolved placeholders, meaning and intended-device listening.
- 08:06–09:26: Authorized voices, disclosure, manual fallback and one-listen feedback.
- 09:26–10:33: Reusable planning pattern, responsibility and learner practice.
- 10:33–11:00: Next lesson and instructor close.

Students may pause the lesson to complete the worksheet and log actual readings; practice duration is not presented as measured lesson time.
