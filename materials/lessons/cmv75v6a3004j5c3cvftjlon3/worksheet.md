# Learner worksheet
## 1. Separate rules from preferences
Read supplied-facts.csv. Write the three non-negotiable constraints: one page, exact dates and times, no added facts. Rank quick phone scanning, obvious actions and warm community tone in that order.

## 2. Request and inspect alternatives
Use prompt.txt in a general assistant, or complete manual-comparison.txt yourself. Request exactly three meaningfully different structures. The lesson's examples are A chronological schedule, B action-first checklist, and C community bulletin. For each use the same criteria and state one main gain and one main sacrifice. Missing locations, contact person and response method must remain unknown. Do not infer actionable instructions or response channels from the word action-first.

## 3. Check before choosing
Check every date and time against the CSV, and inspect whether each proposed layout actually fits one page at your intended font and viewing size. No AI assertion proves one-page fit. Check that three distinct structures use identical criteria, no constraint is broken, both gain and sacrifice are visible, and assumptions/unknowns are labeled. Dates here are fictional exercise facts; for real prices, schedules, rules, availability and permissions inspect current appropriate sources independently.

## 4. Change one priority only
Move warm community tone to first, keeping quick phone scanning before obvious actions. Keep the original facts, constraints and three option structures unchanged. Ask for a revised provisional recommendation. Record whether it moved from B toward C and explain why; genuine responses can differ if well reasoned. This tests sensitivity, not objective truth or measured volunteer behavior.

## 5. Repair duplicates
If the alternatives are too similar, use repair-prompt.txt. Keep the number fixed rather than accumulating repetitive options. Readability and tone are proposals for your judgment, not verified user research.

## 6. Practice independently
Choose a low-stakes task you understand. Supply facts, constraints and two or three ranked priorities. Request a deliberate small number of options, identical comparison fields and a provisional recommendation. Do not enter private information. Use manual fallback whenever an assistant is unavailable, unsuitable or confusing. Higher-consequence decisions need stronger checks and appropriate human expertise.
