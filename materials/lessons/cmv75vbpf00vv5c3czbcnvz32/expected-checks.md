# Expected exercise checks
These are designed answers for fictional inputs, not observed platform results.

- P193-N: form-submission trigger; copy subject/details/contact preference unchanged into a draft and a review note. A suitable draft proposes pointing to a workshop outline but must not invent its URL or claim a message was sent. Human review must resolve where the outline actually is before use.
- P193-M: details are absent. Stop normal drafting or create a clarification record. Flag the missing details; never invent them. Retain the subject and source record.
- P193-U: a purchase and outside send exceed the defined draft-only task. Route to manual handling without purchasing or contacting anyone. No successful external action is recorded.

All three records must retain their original inputs. Required fields, input fidelity, missing details and scope must be checked before any human decision is recorded. A simulated completed run is not evidence that an actual platform has run.

## Arithmetic example (invented assumptions only)
For a hypothetical month: 20 runs × 6 manual minutes = 120 manual minutes. Setup 30 + testing 15 + 20 × (review 2 + correction 0.5 + monitoring 0.25) + maintenance 10 = 110 automation minutes. Difference 10 minutes favoring the estimate only. It does not establish actual savings or justify a risky workflow. At 2 runs with otherwise identical assumptions: 12 manual minutes versus 60.5 automation minutes; staying manual is reasonable. Neither example includes a measured monetary subscription cost.
