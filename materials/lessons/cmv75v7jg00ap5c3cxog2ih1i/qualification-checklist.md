# Consequence and qualification check

Before acting ask:
- Could an error cause serious harm, essential-money loss, missed time or loss of a right?
- Does judgment depend on personal history, location, eligibility, contracts, medicines or conditions not inspected?
- Do current rules, professional standards, manufacturer instructions or workplace controls matter?
- Is there a deadline, possible emergency, irreversible action or conflict of interest?
- Does a proposed reviewer have relevant current qualifications, authority, permitted scope and accountability in the actual location?

Any concern may warrant escalation; this is not a numerical scoring tool and cannot establish that waiting is safe. Possible immediate danger calls for the appropriate urgent official route. Timely consequential review belongs with a suitable qualified person. Low-consequence general information still needs an authoritative source whose date, audience, location and limits fit.

Find the actual local official register or regulator independently. Verify current status and permitted service. Obtain contact details independently of an offer when impersonation is possible. Record real opened sources and dates only when actually checked. Leave fields unresolved when access is missing.

Manual fallback: stop following the generated recommendation; retain originals; write a factual sourced timeline; list constraints without guessing; formulate questions; contact the appropriate service using suitable trusted channels and minimum necessary information.
