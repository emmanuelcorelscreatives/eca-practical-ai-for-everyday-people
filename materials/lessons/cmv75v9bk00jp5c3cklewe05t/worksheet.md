# Project 5 — Build a Checked Decision Brief
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Lesson 120

All providers, offers, observations and buyer details here are fictional, self-authored teaching data. They are not internet offers or measured service reliability. No purchase, message or external commitment is part of this exercise. The numeric practice data supplements the unchanged lecture; do not present it as a claim about a real provider.

## Decision and boundaries
Two adults work from home. The stated monthly service-price budget is 55 currency units; reliability is required and no installation appointment before 08:00 is acceptable. Lower known monthly cost is a preference, not permission to ignore contract conditions. Compare home internet only, not mobile plans or bundles. Equipment fees and total affordability require separate checking. A conditional classroom recommendation is not approval to purchase.

## Files and process
1. Open sources/northstar.html, harbor.html and cedar.html in your browser. Check exact plan, fictional region, page date and conditions. These local source pages are the supplied evidence for the simulation, not live provider websites. Do not use the search snippet as evidence.
2. Complete templates/evidence-log.csv from the opened pages. Record a real date when YOU inspect them; the page's simulated publication date is not your checked date. Keep exact source file and section in each claim.
3. Fill templates/comparison.csv using consistent monthly units and the same service tier. Treat “unknown” as unknown, not zero. Compare against reference/comparison.csv after doing your own work.
4. Open workbook.html locally for a genuine interactive course calculator. Its cells are editable; the visible formula and result update together. Monthly service charge × 12 + known one-time installation fee = first-year KNOWN cost. An unknown equipment fee is visibly excluded. This is a small self-authored teaching app, not Excel or an observed assistant response. Alternatively calculate on paper or enter these values/formulas in your own spreadsheet. Do not claim a complete total for Cedar.
5. Record direct must-have statuses before scoring. Keep evidence, preference, assumption and unresolved question separate. Use templates/decision-brief.md; write conditional advice with a runner-up.
6. Compare Scenario A (lower known monthly price preferred) against Scenario B (installation flexibility preferred), without changing any evidence. Weight fields stay blank until the buyer explicitly supplies meaningful priorities. Never turn unknown into zero or let an average override a failed requirement.
7. Validate your work using reference/answer-key.md and optionally `python3 check_reference.py` from this folder. The command checks supplied arithmetic and missing-value handling only; it does not approve your judgment or verify real offers.

## Deliverables and acceptance
Submit the three-option comparison, claim-level evidence record with checked dates, visible formulas plus units/exclusions, direct requirement statuses, one preference change, and final conditional brief with runner-up and open questions. Include at least one unknown and explain its effect. Label assumptions and hypothetical data. The final decision remains human. Keep all real actions outside the exercise.
