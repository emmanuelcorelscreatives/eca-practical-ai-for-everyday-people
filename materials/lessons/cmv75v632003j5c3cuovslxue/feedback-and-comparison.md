# Focused revision exercise

Use the supplied fictional brief and Draft 1. Preserve Draft 1; never overwrite it.

## Request

“Revise the tone only. Make the opening and transitions warm and direct for new volunteers. Keep the meaning, all supplied facts, 120–150-word length, short opening, and exactly three bullet points unchanged. Do not add missing details.”

You may paste the brief and draft into an assistant you can access, or revise the opening manually in any document editor. Share only these fictional materials. Generated responses vary; the supplied Draft 2 is an Academy authored reference example, not evidence of a particular assistant run. A vague “Make it better” is deliberately not the exercise instruction.

## Deliberate fault, not an event claim

Suppose someone adds “Free parking is available behind the hall.” The brief provides no parking information. Remove this claim or mark parking unknown. Do not treat it as a true arrangement.

Correction request: “Remove the unsupported parking statement. The brief does not provide parking information. Preserve every other sentence and keep the current format unchanged.”

Accept a revision only when the requested weakness improves and protected requirements still hold. If prompting keeps changing correct material, edit the sentence manually.
