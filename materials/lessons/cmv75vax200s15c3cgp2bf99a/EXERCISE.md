# Exercise: prove every critical detail

Use only the fictional notice in this folder. Keep the source intact.

1. Open the original image at a readable size. Find the heading, date, time, place, fee, identifier and both small-print sentences.
2. Extract its text with an available tool, or use the manual fallback below. Save raw output without silently correcting it.
3. Open the separately labelled `intentionally-faulty-extraction.txt`. Compare it line by line with the source. Identify the changed identifier and the missing condition word. Do not use contextual guessing to repair either one.
4. Fill `worksheet.csv`. Record the raw value, the checked source value and the matching line/region. Check date day/month/year, both time digit pairs and colon, B versus 8 and zero versus O, currency and decimal mark, exact reference case/hyphen, and every condition word.
5. Correct a COPY of the extraction using only visible source evidence. Label it “Checked against original image.” This label records your comparison; it does not establish that a real notice is current or authoritative.
6. Compare the original and blurred derivative. Which strokes or small words have become difficult to prove? Use the clean source or take a better permitted image rather than guessing.
7. Optionally compare the actual Linux Tesseract output. Its engine may differ from your phone. Keep any disagreements visible and decide from the original image.

## Manual fallback and accessibility

If extraction is unavailable or unreliable, enlarge the photograph and type only the labelled fields needed. Enter one field, compare immediately, then continue. For unreadable source text write “unclear” and seek a clearer image; never infer missing words. Magnification, a larger display, repeated read-aloud or help from a trusted person can provide a second checking method. Read-aloud may reveal an omission but is not evidence of accurate extraction.

Only after checking the source text should you optionally use read-aloud or translation. After translation recheck names, dates, amounts and identifiers; a capable person may be needed for unfamiliar language. No translated output is supplied or claimed here.
