# Finish a Ready-to-Use Document

Practice the three review passes from Lesson 78 using a fictional community workshop. All notes and examples are self-created; no real event, registration service or contact is represented. Work offline or in your own permitted document editor. No account, AI subscription or external sharing is required. Do not put private information into an assistant.

Start with source-notes.txt, which is the evidence for this exercise. The study fixture is a deliberately flawed draft, not evidence of an actual model response. The corrected DRAFT is a comparison example, not a ready-to-share announcement. Its missing contact remains a publication blocker.

1. Read source-notes.txt. Circle every date, time, place, number, condition and action. Identify the required missing field. Keep the notes open beside your work.
2. Read generated-draft-study-fixture.md. Find the omitted words and explain how their absence changes an attendee's expectation. Optional: supply assistant-instruction.txt to an assistant you are authorized to use. Any actual result is a new draft to check; it may differ from the fixture. Never claim it reproduced the fixture unless observed.
3. Paste only the announcement body into a real document editor. Save an editable copy with DRAFT in its name and keep a visible reminder to compare with the source notes. Do not copy the study-fixture explanation into an event announcement.
4. Structure pass: put the title first, then the short opening, Workshop details and an easy-to-scan list, then Registration and the required action. Answer: Is purpose clear? Is order useful? Can details be found quickly? Is the next action visible?
5. Factual pass: complete factual-checklist.csv one source item at a time. Restore the missing condition. Preserve "if available" as well as "by request". Keep [contact name needed] and leave Registration link blank. Do not invent a person, address, link, promise or contact channel. Do not add a policy for requesting a phone.
6. Destination-formatting pass: inspect actual page size, margins, heading styles, spacing and aligned list indentation. Remove unnecessary blank lines. Keep each heading with its text and inspect the final line and any blank page. Aim for one readable page; do not shrink text until it is unreadable merely to hit one page. Language tools may suggest edits but cannot verify these event facts.
7. Export a practice PDF using the editor's actual available export/save/download/print option. Keep the editable source. Open the file that was actually created, check its page count, and inspect every page from title through final line for clipping, displaced bullets, stranded headings and extra pages. Record the actual result in destination-check-record.md. Menu names and results vary. This kit contains no exported PDF and makes no claim that a page count has been observed.
8. Test real links only if a verified link is later supplied; none is supplied here. Reopen on your intended reading device or service if available. Record unperformed checks as not performed. Stop before sharing: the required contact is missing. Write what a responsible person must supply and which checks must be repeated after that change.

Manual fallback: draft directly from source-notes.txt in your editor, then repeat steps 4–8. The same quality standard applies with or without an assistant.

Compare your reasoning with answers.md after attempting the work. Keep sharing as a separate action under your control. This exercise ends with a corrected, inspected DRAFT that is still held.
