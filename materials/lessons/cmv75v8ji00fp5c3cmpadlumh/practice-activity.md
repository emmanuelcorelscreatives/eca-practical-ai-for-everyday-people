# Practice activity — unchecked worked draft
Use the source, not memory. Four situations contain five answer checks.

1. You arrive at 9:40 a.m. Is that on time? Give the arrival deadline and class start separately.
2. What must you bring and what must you wear? Do not invent equipment.
3. You have a food allergy. Whom must you tell, and when? What warning explains the need for care?
4. Two learners ask: a fifteen-year-old wants to attend without an adult; another learner needs to cancel. State the adult requirement and the exact latest cancellation date. Does the source supply an exact cancellation hour?

Optional Spanish rehearsal: explain one answer in Spanish using the checked glossary. Ask which specific word was difficult to understand; do not request an unsupported fluency score. Record feedback as a rehearsal clue.
