# Choose before creating
| Purpose | Method | Essential check |
|---|---|---|
| Explore a fictional neighborhood reading-day announcement | Generate | Clearly label synthetic; inspect people, objects and text |
| Remove one decoration from a permission-cleared illustration | Edit | Keep original; verify every other meaningful part is unchanged |
| Document the actual condition of an object or printed notice | Real photograph | Full source/context retained; no synthetic replacement |

A realistic synthetic scene is not evidence that the scene happened. An edited source may change meaning even when it looks plausible. Camera capture records what the camera captured, but framing, timing and missing context still matter. Provenance records file history; it is not an independent truth guarantee.
