Practice permission question from the lesson: “May I record a five-minute recap for private notes and delete it when no longer needed?” This is an example of explaining a narrow purpose, not universal legal wording or permission for an entire meeting or wider sharing. Silence is not treated as agreement. Follow stricter applicable rules. If permission changes, stop immediately, clarify handling and follow the applicable agreement, policy and local requirements.

Proceed only when purpose, people, section and use are appropriate. Otherwise use notes-fallback.md.
