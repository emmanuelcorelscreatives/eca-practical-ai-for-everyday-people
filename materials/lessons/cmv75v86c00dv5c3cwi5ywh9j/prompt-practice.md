Explain percentages. I can divide whole numbers, but I do not understand what a percentage represents. My goal is to interpret simple discounts and test scores. ## Authored practice response — not a live model observation
A percentage tells you how many parts there are out of one hundred equal parts. Twenty-five of one hundred equal parts is 25%. A 10% discount on 50 removes 5. Fifteen correct answers out of twenty is 75%. Assumption: you are comfortable dividing whole numbers, as your brief states. Check the arithmetic independently.
