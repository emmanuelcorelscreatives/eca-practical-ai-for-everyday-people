# Choose a Small Useful Automation — Lesson 211
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 36.

Choose a task; do not build or connect an automation in this lesson. All examples are fictional, self-created workshop enquiries. Nothing here sends a message, publishes, purchases, deletes, grants permissions or contacts a service. No AI or paid account is required.

1. Open `worksheet.md` and compare the three candidates in `candidate-comparison.csv`.
2. Read the six sample enquiries in `workshop-enquiries.csv` as text. The dates are fictional requested dates, not bookings.
3. Complete `selection-card-blank.csv`. Compare your reasoning with `selection-card-example.csv` and `answer-key.md`.
4. For the manual fallback exercise, copy one complete record into `review-item-template.md`, preserving every field. Set status to **Needs review**. Compare against the original; do not approve or send it.
5. Use the two intentionally faulty copies in `review-item-checks.csv` to practice checking. They are deliberately incorrect, not approved output.

CSV files are UTF-8, comma separated, with headers. Import as text into a spreadsheet or read with a text editor; no macros, formulas or scripts. Date strings use YYYY-MM-DD to avoid local date ambiguity. These are supplementary practice files, not captures of Google Sheets or proof of any external action.

Files stay local unless you deliberately upload your own practice copy. Replace these fictional examples only with permission-cleared data. Lesson 212 covers building the input and draft steps; it is outside this selection exercise.
