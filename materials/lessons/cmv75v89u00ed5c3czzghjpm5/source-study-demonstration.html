Maple Street Garden Watering Notes

S1 The garden has two 200-liter barrels.

S2 Volunteers collect rainwater from a shed roof and water seedlings on Tuesday and Friday mornings.

S3 During water restrictions, the rainwater is reserved for seedlings and newly planted beds.

S4 The system reduces tap-water use without making the water safe to drink.

S5 Covered barrels limit debris and mosquitoes.
