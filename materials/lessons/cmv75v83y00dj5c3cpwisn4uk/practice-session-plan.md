# Optional 12-minute learner practice session
This is a practice-session plan, not an instruction to stretch, trim, resample or change the model’s native lesson audio. Native narration duration remains authoritative for the actual video.

00:00–01:00 Read fictional notes and identify confirmed/proposed/missing fields.
01:00–03:00 Verify full recipient; compare the faulty draft with opened evidence.
03:00–05:00 Open version-three PDF and inspect both pages; compare distractors.
05:00–07:00 Check dates and proposed action; distinguish sending from receiving, and remove the invented delivery promise.
07:00–09:00 Rewrite the unsent message as a clarification request; do not invent permission or approval.
09:00–11:00 Complete the worksheet and compare the answer key.
11:00–12:00 Perform final recipient/evidence/commitment check and record unresolved details.
Total suggested learner practice time: 12 minutes. No message is sent.
