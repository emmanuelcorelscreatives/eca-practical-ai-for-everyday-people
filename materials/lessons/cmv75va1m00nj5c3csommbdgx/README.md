# Lesson 143 — Create a Simple Work Procedure
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

Use this self-authored fictional low-risk weekly-update pack to turn a recurring task into observable steps. No real workplace, manager, policy, permission or external action is represented. The purpose ends at a reviewed internal draft: sending, publishing and shared-record changes are outside this procedure.

Start with source-notes.md and current-template.md. Follow exercise.md, build your own procedure using procedure-template.md, and compare with worked-procedure.md and answer-key.md. Review-checklists.md gives the two checkpoints. All examples are safe fictional classroom material; do not upload confidential workplace content without explicit local approval of both tool and information.

Keep instructions separate from source notes. An assistant can organize a draft but cannot verify the source independently. Missing approval owner stays blank, marked Confirm locally. Do not mark the draft approved or ready to send because a checklist looks complete.
