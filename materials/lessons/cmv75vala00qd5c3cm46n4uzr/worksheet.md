# Lesson 160 — Edit Images While Preserving Important Details
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Fictional, self-created practice material

## Source and boundary
Use garden-day-original.png from this kit, or a photo you own or have permission to edit. Our course image is synthetic; it depicts a fictional garden event and no real volunteers or incident. It is a practice image, not unaltered real-world evidence. Preserve the original file untouched. Do not imply a synthetic or edited image is an authentic event record.

## Exercise
1. Duplicate the original into a working copy. Record its filename, dimensions and SHA-256 if available.
2. Identify the red disposable cup at lower right. List protected details before requesting an edit.
3. Use edit-brief.txt. In Adobe Firefly or another available AI editor, upload only a copy, select the cup and a narrow margin, and avoid fingers, mug and table edge. Account controls, permissions, plans and regions may differ; check actual access. No product result is guaranteed by this kit.
4. Generate candidate edits and compare each with the untouched original. Use comparison-checklist.md. Reject a successful cup removal if it damages fingers, changes a banner letter, invents objects or breaks texture/shadows.
5. Read banner text against the original: RIVERSIDE GARDEN DAY / 18 OCTOBER. OCR can assist but cannot prove correct text or authenticity.
6. Export an accepted result as garden-day-edited-v1.png, preserving the original. Complete edit-record-template.md with your actual observations, not this lesson's illustrative outcomes.
7. In Canva or another available design editor, place the image in a plain draft poster. Inspect both the image asset and actual destination crop. Correct a crop that hides a wrist, hand or watering can.
8. If no AI result passes, use the reversible manual fallback or keep the original. Do not keep generating merely to avoid reverting.

## What this kit does and does not claim
The source image and written exercises are supplied. No included file claims to be an observed Firefly variation, Google Lens recognition, Canva export or a successful manual repair. Record actual tool outputs yourself. The three variation outcomes described in the authored lesson illustrate rejection/acceptance criteria; your actual results may differ.
