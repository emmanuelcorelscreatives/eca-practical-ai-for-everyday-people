# Lesson 109 — Capture Audio with Permission and Purpose
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

Use this kit to prepare one focused personal recording, inspect its boundaries and choose notes whenever recording is unsuitable. This lesson ends with a scoped audio file, not a transcript, AI summary, confirmed decision or assigned task. Lesson 110 covers transcription.

## Safe practice
1. Read `purpose-and-permission-checklist.md`. Use only your own voice in a private place without confidential material. Do not record a real meeting for this exercise.
2. Copy the three headings from `planning-outline.md` into your notes app. Confirm nobody else is within microphone range.
3. With a recorder of your choice, record `personal-note-script.txt` in your own voice. In the lesson’s iPhone demonstration, Notes holds the outline and Voice Memos provides Record and Stop. Actual app layouts vary; this kit does not claim to provide iPhone screenshots.
4. Stop immediately after the third point. Aim for a short personal note of approximately thirty seconds; do not add irrelevant words merely to meet a duration.
5. Rename your file “Planning questions — permission-safe personal note”. Listen at the beginning, through its contents and near the end. Complete `scope-check.md` against what you actually hear.
6. If you hear unintended background speech, do not use that file for the exercise. Discard the unsuitable personal practice recording and record again in private. Apply relevant agreements and policies to real recordings; do not assume this exercise sets legal rules.
7. Keep the file private in a location appropriate to its purpose. Do not upload/share it automatically. Review whether it remains necessary when the defined task is complete.

## When recording is unsuitable
Open `notes-fallback.md` instead. No recording is required where permission is missing, uncertain or withdrawn, institutional rules prohibit it, or key points alone suffice. Follow applicable local requirements and organizational policy. A platform notification alone does not establish permission for every use.

## Success check
A personal practice file has one intended speaker, a clear purpose, only three relevant planning points, no confidential or third-party content, a useful name, and a prompt stop. A notes-only fallback is a successful outcome where recording is inappropriate. Compare with `worked-example.md`; do not submit private audio to this public repository.
