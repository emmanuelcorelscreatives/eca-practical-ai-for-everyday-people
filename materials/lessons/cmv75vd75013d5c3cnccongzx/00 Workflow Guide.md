## Start here
Make a working copy of this folder called Project 10 - Community Event. Keep the six numbered core files unchanged in your reference copy. Open UTF-8 TXT in an ordinary editor, CSV in Calc or another spreadsheet, and HTML in a browser or document editor. No login, paid feature, macros or internet access is required for the manual route. ## Acceptance and stop conditions
Pass as a reviewed draft only when supported facts and meaning are preserved, the two unsupported fixture statements are removed, unknowns remain visible, permissions are respected and YOUR destination export passes inspection. Stop for critical factual error, missing evidence, conflicting times, private data, unclear permissions or unapproved external action. Revise or take the manual route; never lower the criteria.
