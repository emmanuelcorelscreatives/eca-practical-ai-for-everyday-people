# Reasoning checks — no fabricated account results

There is no predetermined toggle state. Every observation field begins blank. “Not visible” or “Unknown — not tested” can be an honest outcome. Missing controls do not establish an off state.

- A history entry demonstrates a visible saved record, not training eligibility or public access.
- Turning off improvement does not necessarily remove history, erase older chats or reverse previous learning. Read scope and feedback exceptions.
- Archive and delete differ. Finding a chat in archived chats means it was retained.
- Temporary mode can still involve retention. Record the current notice; never assume zero retention.
- A public link may be forwarded. One recipient is not an access restriction. Review the snapshot before creating it.
- A revocation test requires an actual before observation and a refreshed signed-out after observation of the same disposable link. Clicking Remove alone is not a verified result.
- Removing access cannot recall saved copies elsewhere.
- Products may couple activity and improvement differently; removing activity may not remove a separately shared link. Inspect each control.

Offline exercise: placeholders avoid supplied real identities, but do not prove a service is appropriate. Determine all three boundaries separately; if necessary evidence is missing, retain the local note. Do not manufacture screenshots or account observations.

Completion: record date, account category without identifier, actual labels and scope, visible history, temporary-retention wording, sharing decision and actual tests if undertaken. Record uncertainty and the next safe step. Keep private identifiers and URLs out of any submission.
