# Requests to compare

## Underspecified request
Write something about this community event.

Supply the same five fictional notes below it. Diagnose why the result might be too long, omit the exclusion, or invent registration instructions. Those outcomes are possible, not guaranteed.

## Five-part request
GOAL: Turn the supplied notes into a clear announcement for the event.

CONTEXT: The audience is local residents. The announcement will appear on a noticeboard and in a community messaging group.

INPUT:
```text
Riverside Community Hall
Saturday, 14 November
10:00–13:00
Bring clean books, toys, or kitchen items
No electrical items
```

CONSTRAINTS: Use supplied facts only. Keep the tone friendly and direct. Include the exclusion. Mark missing details as [DETAIL NEEDED].

OUTPUT FORMAT: One headline, one introductory sentence, four bullet points, and one closing action. Maximum 100 words.

The source block contains material to transform; the other sections are instructions. The five sections may be shortened, reordered or omitted when they are not useful. This framework is not a universal formula.
