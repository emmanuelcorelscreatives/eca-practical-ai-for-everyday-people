# Seed Swap Visual Brief — fictional training example
## Purpose and intended use
Help local residents notice a free seed-swap event. Portrait social announcement mainly viewed on phones; image sits behind a short headline that will be added manually later. No actual event details or attendance are claimed.

## Subject and action
An open wooden seed box with plain paper packets, gardening gloves and one small hand trowel. A pair of adult hands places one packet into the box. The garden is the setting, not a substitute for a clear main subject. Plain packet surfaces have no readable generated writing.

## Composition and viewpoint
Medium still-life, slight overhead view. Seed box in the lower half; uncluttered upper third reserved for the headline. Keep packets and tools recognizable and preserve useful margins. Do not crop away the subject or headline space.

## Style and references
Warm, realistic editorial photography; natural morning light; believable wood, paper and fabric textures; restrained green and earth colours. Use only self-owned or permission-cleared references. Reference direction: “Use this reference only for warm daylight and muted green colors, not its people or layout.” The kit's native vector diagram is a composition aid, not a claimed photographic lighting reference.

## Dimensions
Portrait 4:5 aspect ratio, 1080 × 1350 pixels. Ratio specifies shape; pixels specify canvas size. If a tool accepts only one value, provide the supported value. Check the destination's current requirements before final export; this exercise does not assert a platform's current size limits.

## Exclude
No logos, brand names, crowds, readable packet text or extra tools. Generate the background without the exact headline; add controlled typography manually in the editor.

## Required baseline, flexible refinements and unknowns
**Required for this baseline:** fictional free seed exchange purpose; open wooden seed box/plain paper packets/gloves/one trowel; adult hands placing one packet; lower-half box/clear upper-third headline area; medium still-life/slight overhead; warm realistic editorial treatment/natural light/believable textures/restrained green-earth palette; portrait4:5,1080×1350 when supported; phone-use readability; all stated exclusions and controlled headline.

**Flexible refinements within that baseline:** exact muted-green/earth shades, natural wood-grain pattern and plain packet arrangement may vary while preserving recognizability, material realism, one-trowel count, action, composition and readability. Later headline font choice may vary only if exact spelling, clear upper space and phone legibility remain intact. None of these preferences permits a different subject, viewpoint, aspect ratio, action or invented event information.

**Unknowns:** date, place and contact stay blank until confirmed. Current destination export rules and any external reference permissions are checked when those sources are selected; do not assume them from this fictional example.
