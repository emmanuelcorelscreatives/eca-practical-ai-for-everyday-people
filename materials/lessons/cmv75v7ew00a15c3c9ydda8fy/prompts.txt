OPTIONAL PROMPTS — paste only fictional source-notice.txt, never private booking data

First prompt:
Summarize this community-room notice in four short bullet points for someone considering a booking. Label any missing information.
[Paste the complete practice source here.]

Comparison prompt:
Compare each sentence of your summary with the supplied notice. Make four columns: Source statement; Summary wording; What was lost; Why it matters. Check who, when, where, under what conditions, and who could be affected. Look beyond signal words to headings and nearby sentences. Do not infer missing facts.

Revision prompt:
Revise the summary using only the supplied notice. Restore conditions that affect eligibility, confirmation, hours, supervision, accessibility, food, or same-day requests. Label unresolved information.

Final human check:
Match each material source condition to the revised text. Record preserved or deliberately omitted, with a reason. Check every added assertion against the source. A requested correction is not evidence that a model made it. Actual responses vary; flawed-summary.txt and revised-summary.txt are fixed authored examples only.
