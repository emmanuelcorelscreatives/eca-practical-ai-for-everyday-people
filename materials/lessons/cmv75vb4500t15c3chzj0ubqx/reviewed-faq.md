# Fictional Repair Café — reviewed practice FAQ

TRAINING EXAMPLE — not a real event. Only the three scenario-approved answers are included.

## Do I need to book?

No booking is required. Visitors are helped in arrival order, subject to volunteer availability.

## What time does it start?

The event is on Saturday, 10:00–14:00, at Community Hall.

## Can I bring a toaster?

You may bring a small portable toaster for assessment. Whether volunteers can work on it depends on available skills, tools, and safe working conditions.

## Ask the event team

Can’t find your answer? Contact the event team and include the item type, your question, and any access requirement. A team member will review your request.
