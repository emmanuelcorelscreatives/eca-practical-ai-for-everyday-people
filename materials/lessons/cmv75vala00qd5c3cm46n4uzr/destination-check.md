# Poster destination check
Create a plain fictional draft poster in Canva or another available design editor. Use only RIVERSIDE GARDEN DAY and 18 OCTOBER as the supplied event text; no venue, organizer contact, time or live invitation is provided. Do not invent those details or publish a real event announcement.

Place the accepted edited image, or the original if no edit passes. First inspect the whole image at fit-to-canvas. Test how a narrow crop can hide a wrist or watering can; restore a wider crop before acceptance. Check the normal viewing-size poster and zoom where image and text meet.

Record the actual destination crop and any unresolved concern. Keep a note that the image is synthetic practice material and, if accepted, was edited to remove a disposable cup. Do not claim an actual Canva output exists until you have created and checked it.
