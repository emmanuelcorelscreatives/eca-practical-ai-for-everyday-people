# Procedure: Prepare the Weekly Team-Update Draft
Self-authored fictional classroom example · unverified until checked

## Purpose
Prepare a reviewed internal weekly-update draft from approved notes using the current template. Stop at the saved review copy. Sending, publishing, bookings and shared-record changes are not included.
## Inputs
Reporting period; audience; confirmed deadline; approved task notes; current template; applicable approved local procedure; sharing boundary. Confirm approval owner locally before any approval. Missing facts remain labelled.
## Steps
1. Confirm reporting period and deadline against the source brief. Completion: both copied accurately with unresolved differences recorded.
2. Open the current template. Completion: four required headings are present: Completed, In progress, Blocked, Decisions needed.
3. Sort approved notes under the required headings. Completion: each item retains its source ID. Put ambiguous/unmapped items in a review area.
4. Mark unclear items for confirmation. Completion: N3 remains “Supplier date not confirmed”; approval owner remains blank with “Confirm locally.”
5. Draft concise wording without adding claims. Completion: each statement traces to a source note; no invented delivery date, commitment or status.
6. Save a review copy. Completion: reopen the file and inspect headings and text. Do not send or publish.
## Review Point 1 — Before Drafting
Confirm correct period, deadline, approved notes, current template and sharing boundary. Compare with the task brief, never the assistant answer. Remove private or unrelated details.
## Exception Path
Missing input: stop and request it. Conflicting notes: retain both versions and ask the responsible person. Unmapped content: keep in a review area; do not force it into the approved four headings. Do not invent a policy or approver.
## Review Point 2 — Before Approval
Trace every statement to notes. Compare names, dates, quantities, status words, commitments and meaning. Missing information stays visible. Check the current template, absence of unrelated private data, and actual saved-file readability. Approval owner must be confirmed locally; this exercise leaves it unresolved, so approval remains pending.
## Manual Fallback
If the assistant is unavailable or unsuitable, open the template, sort approved notes manually, preserve unknowns as questions, complete both review lists, and save a review copy. Use the locally approved route when external tools are prohibited.

End status: source-comparison exercise draft; approval owner unresolved; no external action included. A task-knowledgeable colleague should test whether inputs, order, stop conditions and responsible contacts are understandable. Accept only confirmed corrections and retain the source notes.
