# Apply the routine beyond a PDF

These are fictional paper scenarios; no live slide/sheet/image result is claimed.

A. A three-slide community briefing exports as two slides; the remaining slides look polished. List the first check, corrected export scope and recheck. What private material might remain in speaker notes?
B. A sheet has rows for 24 participant badges and 3 volunteer badges, but its printed range excludes the volunteer row. What numbers, formula references, hidden rows, units and print boundaries would you inspect? Why is a displayed total alone insufficient?
C. An event image reads “registration optional” while its source says required. Its rightmost character also disappears at phone width. Separate the meaning defect from the layout defect. What actual destination evidence would support a correction?
D. The corrected PDF has two pages and 27 refreshments, but no real registration destination. Which fact remains unknown? Write a concise visible label without inventing a URL, then state the limitation on real distribution.

Suggested checks: A compare expected/found slide counts, export all intended slides, open saved result, check order and notes; B recompute 24+3, inspect the exact included cells and printed range, open the print/PDF output; C compare source meaning, repair text/edge and inspect the real image at its destination; D registration address unknown, “Registration link: to be added.” A warning does not create a working registration process.
