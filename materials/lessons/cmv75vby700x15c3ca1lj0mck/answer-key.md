# Expected observations

A. Accessibility details and contact method are not supplied; reviewer authority has not been confirmed, and decision/time are unrecorded. Do not invent replacements. An incomplete request cannot invite approval.

B. `event-brief.json` supplies all five event facts; `venue-confirmation.md` separately supports the fictional location/date/time/free entry. The banner matches these records but is not a new independent real-world authority.

C. “Publish community garden announcement” plus the exact non-public test page and explicit scope communicates the consequence. “Continue” alone does not.

D. A material destination/content/attachment change needs a new version and fresh review. Do not silently carry the old authorization.

E. The action remains Disabled when decision is blank, expired, unavailable, rejected or unresolved. This lesson's practice app never publishes even after a local practice click.

F. Manual Pending Review packet: action, destination, full draft, actual attachment, opened supporting records, assumptions, missing information, version, responsible reviewer and explicit recorded decision/time. A draft alone is insufficient.
