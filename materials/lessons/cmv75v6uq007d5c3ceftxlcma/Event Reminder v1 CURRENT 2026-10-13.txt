Event Reminder Template
Status: Current
Current version: v1
Revised: 2026-10-13
Purpose: Turn supplied event notes into a friendly reminder.
Required inputs: Event; Date; Time; Location; Audience; What to bring.

INSTRUCTION
Using only SUPPLIED NOTES, write a friendly reminder of 30 to 60 words. Include the event, date, time, location, audience, and what to bring. If a required detail is absent, write 'Missing information:' and name the missing field. Do not invent names, contact details, promises, or extra event facts. Format: short heading, one paragraph, then a Bring list.

QUALITY CHECK
[ ] Every fact matches the supplied notes.
[ ] Every required item appears.
[ ] No unsupported detail was added.
[ ] Length is 30 to 60 words.

SUPPLIED NOTES — Fictional practice material
Event: Seed-sorting session

Seed-Sorting Session — 2027-04-18
