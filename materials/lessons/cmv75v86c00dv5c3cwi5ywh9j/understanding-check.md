# Try without copying
1. Explain what 25% means in your own words.
2. Forty of one hundred equal items are selected. What percentage is selected?
3. A 10% discount applies to 50. How much is removed? Verify with arithmetic.
4. Fifteen answers out of twenty are correct. What percentage is correct?
5. Which is better evidence of understanding: recognizing a familiar explanation or applying the idea to a fresh example? Explain why.
6. An assistant uses an undefined term. Write one targeted follow-up that preserves the useful discount example.
7. A source notebook gives a citation marker. What must you open and compare before treating it as support?
