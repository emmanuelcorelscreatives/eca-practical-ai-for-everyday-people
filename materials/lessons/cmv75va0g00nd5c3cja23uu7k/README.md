# Lesson 142 — Practise Interviews and Feedback
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

This is entirely fictional, Academy-authored, permission-cleared interview practice. It is not a job advertisement, hiring prediction, candidate assessment or claim about any real person's qualifications.

## Practice sequence
1. Open `fictional-role.md` as ROLE EVIDENCE. Keep your own notes under MY EVIDENCE in `preparation-blank.csv`.
2. Use `coach-instruction.md` with an assistant you are permitted to use, or practise manually. Paste only information you are allowed to share.
3. Answer the question yourself before requesting feedback. Do not submit a model-written answer as your own experience.
4. Check clarity, relevance and supporting evidence separately using the worked fictional example. Fill unknown responsibility/result details only from your own direct evidence.
5. Use `star-template.md` to reorganise an answer. Unknown results remain “result not recorded”.
6. Keep three questions and chosen revisions in `practice-log-blank.csv`. Compare your process against `expected-checks.md`.

The included offline practice app shows the exact source's fixed fictional examples and coaching. Its labels identify these as scripted training material, not output from a live AI or a real hiring decision. It lets you type and revise answers, review evidence and rehearse without uploading personal information. No account, recording, external API or employer service is used. Local typed answers are not persisted and disappear when the page closes.

For real interview preparation, replace fictional examples only with personally supportable facts. Remove any invented achievement, statistic, qualification or team result presented as your individual work. An assistant's agreement is not independent proof.
