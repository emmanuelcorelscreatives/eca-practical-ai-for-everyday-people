## Meaning checklist

## Term exercise (separate illustrative sentence)
In this separate fictional example the source explicitly identifies the substrate as soil. In that context, “moisture-retention substrate” → “soil that holds water.” Without that context, “growing material that holds water” avoids narrowing the meaning. Explain why a necessary technical term may need to remain with an explanation. This term is not secretly part of the watering source.

## Format/listening check
Can the reader find the action on a small screen? Are headings descriptive? Is the exception next to the rule? Are warnings written in words? If you use text-to-speech, note actual missing words or ambiguous phrasing; do not invent an observation.

## Manual fallback
Rewrite using the source and checklist without a tool. Mark unresolved details instead of guessing.
