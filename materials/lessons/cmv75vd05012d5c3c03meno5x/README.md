# Plan a Sensible Tool Budget — Lesson 232
All examples are fictional, self-authored practice records. They describe no real accounts, paid plans, current provider offers, purchases or cancellation actions. No current prices or quotas are supplied. Use ordinary files and any spreadsheet application; no account or payment is needed.
Start with worksheet.md. Open activity-log.csv and fill account-checklist-blank.csv; compare your work with expected-checks.md only after trying. Full-cost-evidence.csv keeps unknown money amounts blank. Keep time and money separate; do not assign a monetary value to time unless you explicitly choose and label an assumption.
The AI Workflows directory contains portable prompt examples, source examples, a checked manual output and cost records. It is a teaching folder rather than an observed operating-system screenshot. Make a backup using your usual permitted method; this kit does not claim a backup has occurred.
Do not enter passwords, full payment details, private documents or third-party personal data. Local storage remains subject to your responsibilities. You may reuse and adapt all self-authored kit content under CC0 1.0; no vendor assets are included.
