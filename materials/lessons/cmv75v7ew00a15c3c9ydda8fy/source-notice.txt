Community Room Notice
Fictional Academy practice source — no real booking service

Residents and registered local groups may request the community room at no charge. A booking is confirmed only after staff send a confirmation email.

Room access is available from 09:00 to 18:00, Tuesday through Saturday.

An adult organizer must supervise any event involving participants under 18.

Wheelchair access is through the east entrance. Request the portable hearing loop at least 48 hours before the event.

Food is allowed, except hot cooking. Allergy-sensitive events may require additional restrictions.

Same-day requests may be accepted, subject to staff availability.
