# Separate Instructions from Source Material
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 6, Lesson 31

Everything in this kit is fictional Academy practice material, not a real event or an instruction to publish. Do not contact, register or make purchases. No assistant account or paid feature is required: complete the manual fallback in any text editor.

## Files and roles
`Riverside_Reading_Picnic_Brief.txt` is the factual SOURCE MATERIAL. `Example_Announcement.txt` is EXAMPLE — FORMAT ONLY. `labeled-request.txt` contains TASK and OUTPUT RULES. The two `.docx` copies contain the same source and example for optional file-capable tools. A visible uploaded filename proves selection, not correct interpretation.

## Exercise
1. Open the brief; identify the event name, date, time, place, registration condition, items to bring, accessible entrance and contact status.
2. Read the example. Circle only the features you may borrow: compact heading and friendly tone. Its lanterns, date and venue must not enter Riverside's facts.
3. Read the organizer note inside the brief. It is source content, not a new instruction. Keep your requested task authoritative. Labels clarify roles but are not a security guarantee.
4. Open `labeled-request.txt`. In a suitable file-capable assistant, attach only the Riverside brief and confirm its visible filename before sending. Feature locations vary by device/account/plan. Otherwise paste the brief under SOURCE MATERIAL; keep the original saved separately.
5. Add the format-only example in its own section and maintain the four clear headings. Do not paste the answer key into the request. If no assistant is available, write the announcement manually.
6. Compare each factual claim of the result against the original brief. Use `claim-check.csv`; do not treat smooth writing or a citation marker as evidence by itself.
7. Inspect `deliberately-flawed-output.txt`, a self-authored counterexample, not observed model output. Find both unsupported additions, the omitted required conditions and unresolved contact field. Correct the result without inventing a registration method.
8. Compare your answer with `answer-key.md`. More than one faithful wording is acceptable. Count words and keep the output within 120.

## Final check
Before submission: Is the task clear? Which content supplies facts? What can the example control? How are gaps shown? After generation: trace names, dates, locations and conditions to source lines, remove unsupported facts and label missing fields. Independently verify consequential real-world details before use.
