# Draft, review, transfer, pause
1. Read source-notes.txt. Identify the supplied date, time, request and attachment reference. Write the missing recipient address in the worksheet as MISSING, not as a guessed address. Do not open the later contact card yet.
2. Optionally paste draft-prompt.txt into a text-only assistant you are authorized to use, with no connected mail tools. Otherwise use unsent-working-draft.txt as the self-authored teaching example. Record whether your text is generated output or the supplied example. An AI response is proposed wording, not evidence of a completed communication.
3. Copy the proposed wording to a local editor as an UNSENT WORKING DRAFT. Check the wording against the original notes. Keep the address placeholder and booking uncertainty. Reject a sentence such as “Thank you for confirming our booking”; replace it with the exact availability question in the answer guide.
4. Mark the text READY TO TRANSFER — NOT APPROVED TO SEND. The recipient, actual attachment and final approval are still unchecked. Complete the meaning-review section of the worksheet.
5. Practice the destination check offline in the worksheet, or in an isolated new mail draft if safe. Start with an empty To field. Do not use autocomplete or real contacts. Now reveal approved-contact-record.txt and compare every character of the full fictional address. For offline practice write it only in the worksheet; leave the actual mail draft recipient empty to prevent accidental dispatch.
6. Locate revised-room-layout.pdf. Verify its filename and open it to confirm its content. In offline practice record “file located; attachment NOT observed.” If you choose to practice attaching it to an isolated draft, verify the tile/filename there. A line mentioning a document is not attachment evidence.
7. Use the final-destination checklist: correct account, full recipient(s), subject, date, time, attachment, request, commitments, tone and approval. In this exercise the final human decision is DO NOT SEND. Keep that decision unchecked until you explicitly record the practice stop. If another person must approve, save a draft and request approval separately; saving is not approval.
8. Read permission-review.md. Complete all three access-capability rows without connecting an app. Finish with a saved local draft or an isolated saved mail draft, never a sent message. Record “Practice ends here — NOT SENT.” Delete any temporary mail draft once you have finished. Keep your private accounts, existing messages and other people's contact details out of shared work.

Success: supplied facts remain accurate, missing information stays visible until a supplied source resolves it, no false booking exists, local-file existence and actual attachment observation are distinguished, and nothing is sent.
