# Manual practice and fallback
You do not need a presentation assistant. Work in an ordinary editor using only these new fictional Academy files.

1. Open source-notes.md. Copy its complete facts into a separate working note; leave registration unconfirmed.
2. Complete the five lines in brief-template.md. Add current situation and language guidance where useful.
3. Explain who the message serves, what should be understood, and what response is reasonable. If your answer is “everyone” or only “community repair,” clarify it first.
4. Write an opening title and supporting details manually. Do not build a detailed multi-slide outline yet; the next lesson covers outlines.
5. Keep the unknown registration method visible and avoid unsupported instructions.
6. Apply review-checklist.md and compare with answer-key.md. Save your brief and checked fictional draft separately from the original source.

Optional transfer task: write a separate brief for experienced repair volunteers. Preserve every event fact and the registration gap. You may choose a different communication emphasis, but the source provides no volunteer recruitment requirements, duties or contact method. Do not invent them. Explain which planning decisions changed and which evidence stayed fixed.

Expected outcome: a useful five-line brief plus optional supporting fields; a short checked opening; and a visible unknown. Completion is demonstrated by clear communication decisions, not decorative quality or access to a particular product.
