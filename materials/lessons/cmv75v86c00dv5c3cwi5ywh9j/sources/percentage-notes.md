# Academy source page A — Percentages
Original educational source by Emmanuel Corels Academy.

A percentage expresses a number of parts per one hundred equal parts. Therefore, 25 out of 100 equal parts is 25%, and 40 out of 100 equal parts is 40%.

To express a score as a percentage, divide correct answers by total answers and multiply by 100. Fifteen correct answers out of twenty gives 15 ÷ 20 × 100 = 75%.

A 10% discount on 50 removes 5 because 50 ÷ 10 = 5. These examples make no claim about a currency, a real shop, or a real person's marks.
