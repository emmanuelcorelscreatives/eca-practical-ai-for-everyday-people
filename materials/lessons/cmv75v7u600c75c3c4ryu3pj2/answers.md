2. Four-factor brief: established working contact (hypothetical), obtain confirmation, clear/courteous expectation, meaningful deadline without emergency. Friendly is a reasonable choice. Direct can work for active preparation; formal can suit first contact or an official record. Multiple choices pass with a reason grounded in the actual brief.
3. Warmth cues: “Hi,” “could you please,” “Thank you.” Formal cues: “Dear,” “I am writing to request,” “Kind regards,” and no contractions. Action cues: “please confirm” and early deadline. These alter expression, not facts. The three full lesson examples are in authored-drafts.json and the practice page.
4. For each version, all names, dates, room, request and reason remain intact.
