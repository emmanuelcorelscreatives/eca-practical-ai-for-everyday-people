# Fictional visual brief
Community Repair Afternoon.
Audience: local families.
Purpose: invite attendance.
Subject: people repairing small household items at a shared table.
Tone: welcoming and practical.
Format: square social post.
Must show: readable title, date field, location field, clear contrast.
Must not claim a real event.

The subject must stay obvious when the square post is small. Welcoming is a preference; a readable title is a requirement. Empty date/location fields are intentional because no real details were supplied. The manual object-only SVGs are composition exercises; they do not fully satisfy the people/activity part of this brief. Do not mark brief compliance Pass for those examples merely because they look tidy.
