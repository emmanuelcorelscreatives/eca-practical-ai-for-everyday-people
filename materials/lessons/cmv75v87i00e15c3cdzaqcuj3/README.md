# Lesson 86 — Use Analogies and Worked Examples
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 15

This practice pack matches the lesson's percentage analogy. It supplies the permission-cleared source note, a complete request, a mapping worksheet, a checked worked example, a hidden-answer fresh task, and a separate answer key. All examples are authored course practice; they are not observations from a student's private account. No account, subscription or AI assistant is required for the manual route.

## Work through the pack
1. Read `percentage-basics.md` first. It is an Academy-authored study note, not an assistant-generated answer or an external authority.
2. Copy `assistant-request.txt` into your chosen assistant if you have one. Treat its reply as generated and check it yourself. Different outputs are expected.
3. Fill `analogy-worksheet.md` using the mapping and its limits. Compare with the study note; keep source wording separate from your interpretation.
4. Study `worked-example.md`. Recompute its arithmetic using a calculator or the included Python checker.
5. Attempt `fresh-practice.md` without opening `answer-key.md`. Keep the analogy out of view.
6. Open the answer key only after writing your reasoning. Revise or discard any comparison that misleads you.

## Reproducible arithmetic check
Before the fresh attempt, run `python3 verify_examples.py` (Python 3, standard library only). Expected result: `PASS: worked example and boundary checks passed.` This checks only the already-shown worked example and analogy boundaries; it does not reveal or inspect the fresh answer.

Only AFTER writing your own fresh attempt, open `answer-key.md`. You may then run `python3 verify_examples.py --reveal-fresh` to verify the separate `answer-key-data.json` as well. That optional command reveals the fresh result. Keep `answer-key-data.json` closed until your attempt is complete. The checker verifies arithmetic, not an AI response or your understanding.

## Source notebook option
Upload only `percentage-basics.md` to a source notebook you are allowed to use. Check the uploaded title and full contents.
