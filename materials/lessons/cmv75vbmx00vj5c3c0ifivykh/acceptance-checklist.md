# Same final acceptance checklist for both routes

- Title, date, time, location, audience and requested action match the opened source.
- A missing location remains explicitly “Location not provided.”
- Conflicting times remain both visible and marked for confirmation; do not choose one.
- Unclear scan characters and date remain uncertain until resolved from real evidence.
- No invented venue, character, date, requested action or unsupported factual claim.
- Readable final artifact; relevant formatting errors corrected before stopping.
- Generated draft is labelled; source evidence and final accepted artifact remain distinct.
- Record pass or revise, material errors, uncertainty, fallback, and final route.

A final pass means the reviewed final artifact met these checks. It does not mean
the first generated draft was flawless. Record earlier mistakes even after correction.
