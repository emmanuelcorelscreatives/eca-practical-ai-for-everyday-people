# Dimensions, destination and crop checks
- Aspect ratio is width:height. 1080:1350 reduces exactly to 4:5; do not confuse it with 5:4.
- In this exercise the portrait canvas is 1080 pixels wide and 1350 high. A square1:1 or widescreen16:9 canvas needs different framing/crop checks.
- Current platform/file/print requirements must be checked at the actual destination before final export. No universal DPI or platform limit is supplied.
- Inspect the final file at realistic phone display size: exact headline readable, seed box not cut off, upper-third type space retained, required edges within margins.
- A website banner or printed notice can require different framing, resolution and margins. Do not assume one crop works everywhere.
- Record final dimensions and actual inspected crop/preview, not only the requested values.
