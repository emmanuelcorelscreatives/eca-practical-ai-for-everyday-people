# Review History, Sharing and Data Settings
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 3, Lesson 15

Separate three questions: is the conversation saved; who can open a shared version; may future content improve models? This kit supplies a fictional book-club prompt, a blank audit, a revocation test log and a local-only fallback. It contains no credentials, real personal information, completed account observations or promise that an interface will be identical.

Start with learner-guide.md. Complete privacy-audit.csv and, only if you choose the disposable-link exercise, link-test-log.csv. Check your reasoning with expected-checks.md. Keep completed observations local; never upload private account screenshots to the public course repository. Account settings do not make inappropriate inputs safe.
