# Worked example — Find 25% of 80
1. Meaning: 25 per 100 of the quantity 80.
2. Convert: 25% = 25 / 100 = 0.25.
3. Multiply: 0.25 × 80 = 20.
4. Result: 25% of 80 is 20.
5. Independent check: 25% is one quarter. 80 / 4 = 20, agreeing with the calculation.
6. Visual check: four equal groups of 20 total 80; selecting one group selects one quarter. The real quantity contains 80, not 100, objects.

Do not treat the attractive 100-tile representation as proof. Check the calculation and compare the definition with the source separately.
