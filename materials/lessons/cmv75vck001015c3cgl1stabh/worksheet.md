# Lesson 218 — Use a Small Approved Knowledge Pack
Emmanuel Corels Academy. All event information in this kit is fictional, self-created practice data. It is not a real event announcement. No external action has occurred.

## Prepare
Open the six files in `Candidate material`. Use `document-register.csv` to inspect their origin, date, purpose and approval. Select only Event Information 2026.pdf and Visitor FAQ 2026.docx; keep the obsolete, unapproved, internal and irrelevant files outside the active pack. The active pack contains two files, not three. Keep operating-instructions.txt separate from source facts.

## Practice
If your service offers projects or custom assistants, confirm its current instruction/file controls and your permission to use them. Create a new course-only Riverside Learning Day workspace. Add the operating instructions separately; upload only the two approved sources. Observe actual upload completion and any preview/index status before asking visitor-question.txt. Product availability varies; this kit makes no claim that an upload or response has already occurred.

Record each important answer claim in evidence-check.csv. Open the original document at the named heading and compare the exact words. A citation label alone is not evidence. Missing information is not a prohibition or permission. Do not invent a booking promise or organizer contact details.

## Manual fallback
Without project features, open the approved originals, copy operating-instructions.txt into a normal chat under OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS, paste the relevant source passages under APPROVED SOURCE EXCERPTS with filenames/headings, and put the visitor question under VISITOR QUESTION. If you do not use an assistant at all, answer manually from the originals using the same evidence check. Never paste private notes.

## Maintenance
Use maintenance-register.csv. Review dates start blank: choose a task-appropriate review point rather than pretending a review occurred. Replace sources only after opening and approving the actual replacement. Remove superseded material from the active pack, retain it in an archive, update the register, then rerun affected questions. This kit supplies no invented newer event version.

## Completion
Check expected-checks.md after completing your own answer. Explain why 09:00 and a guaranteed reservation are unacceptable, and why 'the pack does not say' differs from 'reservations are forbidden'. Keep facts in documents and behavior rules in instructions.
