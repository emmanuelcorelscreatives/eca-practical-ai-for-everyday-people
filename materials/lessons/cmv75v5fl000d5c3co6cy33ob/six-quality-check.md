# Choose a low-risk experiment
For each candidate, consider:
- Small scope: is the intended output modest and clearly bounded?
- Low consequence: would an error be harmless and easy to notice and fix?
- Reversible: can I edit or discard it without changing an important record?
- Non-sensitive input: do I own or have permission to use this material?
- Easy to check: can I trace every output item back to the original notes?
- No automatic action: is this a private draft, without sending, publishing, buying, deleting or changing shared information?

Do not add numerical scores or treat these prompts as a scientific safety measure. If two tasks seem similar, prefer the one that stays private and creates a draft. You decide what is useful in your actual context.
