# Final inspection
- [ ] Origin is clear: synthetic concept, edited copy, or actual camera photograph.
- [ ] Text checked against the authoritative source, including names, numbers and dates.
- [ ] Faces, hands, objects, shadows, edges, patterns and geometry inspected.
- [ ] No misleading documentary claim, real logo or invented event evidence.
- [ ] Permission confirmed; private information excluded.
- [ ] Original source retained unchanged; exact edit and actual change record saved.
- [ ] Full-page photograph retained before crops; blank date remains blank.
- [ ] Raw OCR retained; checked transcript and uncertainties kept separately.
- [ ] Manual editing/transcription or recapture fallback ready.

Write the purpose, audience, constraints and checks for the next visual brief. Do not mark a practice step complete until you actually performed it.
