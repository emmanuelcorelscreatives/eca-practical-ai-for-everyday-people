# Evidence before another subscription
1. Open the blank account checklist and activity log. Count drafting sessions, document-editing sessions and simple image tasks. Record the period; leave recent use blank until you have evidence. Do not equate opening an app with using its AI capability.
2. Fill the three accounts. Add Possible upgrade. Map by results: text drafting, document editing, current-source search, crop and resize. Mark possible overlap between the assistant and office bundle without concluding that equivalent features are available. Keep generated drafts distinct from opened current evidence.
3. Define the gap: review long permission-cleared documents. Record file-size limits and account availability as Unverified. Fill the full-cost evidence sheet only from actual relevant account pages when available. In this offline exercise keep all money, currency, dates and quotas blank. No provider claim is implied.
4. Compare fixed, included and usage-based cost types. In the fictional worked example these correspond to assistant, office bundle and editor. Keep subscription, extra usage and storage charges separate. Check billing period, taxes, region/eligibility and included features. Annual and monthly totals are not interchangeable; avoid double-counting a bundle. Unknown costs mean no defensible total yet.
5. Use the blank workflow comparison for one permission-cleared task in your own permitted environment. Record draft time plus checking and corrections separately for existing setup, proposed upgrade if available, and manual baseline. Never fill measured cells with guesses. Mark an unavailable option untested. A quicker draft alone does not prove a better total workflow.
6. Open AI Workflows/Prompts/Rewrite message.txt and its source example. Complete the short announcement manually in an ordinary document. Compare with the checked manual output; no AI provider is required. Keep a browser/source-check and calculator available for tasks that need them. A fallback succeeds only when it safely completes the same task.
7. Verify where each subscription was purchased and record renewal and cancellation location without signing in or cancelling for this exercise. Mobile marketplace purchases may use phone subscription settings. Deleting an app does not establish cancellation. Real cancellation is complete only after you confirm status through the proper account route; save permitted confirmation without payment secrets.
8. Choose Keep, Downgrade, Cancel or Test later with a reason. For the editor record Test later with next action Test manual fallback; the narrated visual phrase “Test manual fallback” is an action, not a fifth menu choice. Retain the assistant for recurring drafts while no equivalent is confirmed. Postpone upgrade until file limits, availability, full cost and advantage are verified. Do not invent an office-bundle cancellation decision.
9. Check the four purchase questions: used recently, genuine capability gap, full cost known, workable fallback. Pause a purchase if key evidence is missing. Keep your completed checklist with prompts and workflow records; review after normal use or changed responsibilities/plans. Paid features do not remove independent output checks.
