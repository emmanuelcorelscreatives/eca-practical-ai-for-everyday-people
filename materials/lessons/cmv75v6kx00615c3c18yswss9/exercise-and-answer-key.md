## Boundaries

Scope sets coverage, detail sets explanation depth, tone shapes wording, structure sets order/format. These are practical labels, not universal taxonomy. Holding the source, criterion and other instructions fixed makes comparisons interpretable; it does not eliminate model variability or prove single-sentence causation. No factual accuracy follows solely from clearer style. Choose a real destination (poster/email/message) only after checking facts and criteria; this exercise performs none of those external actions.
