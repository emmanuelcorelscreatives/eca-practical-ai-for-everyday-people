# Expected checks and worked reasoning

- Decision: choose a suitable venue for a three-hour community workshop.
- Constraint set: available on event date; legally permitted capacity at least 40; step-free route to workshop room. All option statuses remain Unknown because no evidence is supplied. No option has been ruled in or out.
- Five initial criteria: total cost, travel time, accessibility beyond minimum, room suitability, cancellation flexibility. Final worked set adds public transport practicality only after the decision maker accepts it.
- Total cost: all required charges for the same booking, required equipment, mandatory fees and applicable taxes; exclude optional extras not requested. Choose and state one currency; do not invent amounts.
- Travel time: one-way minutes from Central Library entrance, same mode and comparable period. Practicality: frequency and final walking route. These have distinct consequences.
- Accessibility: the minimum step-free room route is not rewarded again; record confirmed additional features individually. “Accessible” alone does not prove any specific feature or justify a score.
- Room suitability: Before assessment, agree explicit requirements for seating layout, presentation space and planned activity space. Meets: all three requirements confirmed met. Partly meets: one or two confirmed met and the others confirmed not met. Does not meet: all three confirmed not met. Unknown: any requirement or its evidence is missing. Never override a failed pass-or-stop constraint. With no agreed activity specification or layout evidence, no venue category can be assigned.
- Cancellation: opened deadline, fee, refund conditions and exceptions; promotional language is not evidence.
- Suggestions: separate, unaccepted until manual decision. Example acceptance of practicality and rejection of environmental impact are bounded choices, not universal advice. Availability remains a constraint.
- Shared basis: same booking duration, origin, mode, period, activities, evidence standard. Event date, currency, mode, period and activities require agreement before actual evidence collection.
- CSV check: six criteria in worked example and six columns; evidence preview has three fictional options, all 18 result cells exactly Unknown. No ranking, weight, score or winner field.
- Offline application: no network or AI call; changes in Practice do not silently change teaching states or persist private data. Print/copy practice work before closing.

Self-check: if you can explain each boundary without choosing a venue, and preserve every unknown instead of inventing a value, the exercise is complete. Different reasonable priorities are acceptable if explicit, nonoverlapping and consistently measured. This lesson does not teach weighting or ranking.
