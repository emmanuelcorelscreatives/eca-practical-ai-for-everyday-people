# Project 7: Create a Checked Communication Kit

This kit follows Lesson 168 with the exact fictional Riverside Reading Circle facts. It supplies working source documents, master facts, square design, generic synthetic speech, timed captions, three actual exports and a deliberate caption/wording error exercise. The supplied original **digital illustration** is an explicitly labelled alternative because the saved project contained no original permission-cleared photograph. For the lesson's photograph track, take your own image and complete Permission_Note; never mislabel the supplied illustration.

1. Read 01_Source_Pack/Event_Brief and Permission_Note. Keep every unavailable detail unresolved.
2. Compare the fact sheet and information sheet field by field.
3. Inspect the SVG/PNG at phone-like size. Replace only the illustration with your own permission-cleared photo if following the photo track.
4. Listen to Narration_v1.wav while reading Media_Script. Generic eSpeak is a reproducible fallback, not the course instructor. You may record your own voice and retime captions.
5. Compare draft-incorrect.srt with corrected.srt; explain why D must become B. Open the MP4 and watch every second; do not assume transcript implies accurate audio.
6. Open the actual PDF/PNG/MP4, fill Review_Checklist.csv, and record corrections. Do not publish this practice event.

Manual fallback: any ordinary document editor, image editor, authorized human voice and manually timed captions can produce the same three artifacts. LibreOffice opens the provided HTML or ODT sources. No paid service or account is required for the included example. Rebuild: see rebuild.md.
