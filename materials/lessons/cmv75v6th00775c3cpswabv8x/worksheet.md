# Include a Quality Check in Every Template
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 8, Lesson 45

## Purpose
Save task-specific evidence and acceptance criteria beside the reusable instruction. This supplemental practice kit supports the authored lesson; it does not assert that a completed video, external model run or published event has been verified.

## Files and exercise order
1. Open `source-notes.txt`, a wholly fictional Lantern Library Club case.
2. Copy `announcement-template.txt` into any notes app or document. Fill audience and destination; keep instruction and quality check together.
3. Compare the deliberately faulty and corrected examples in `worked-announcements.md` against the source. Record each finding yourself.
4. Complete `three-templates-worksheet.md`: announcement, expense total and source summary. Use a real calculator/spreadsheet for the independent total.
5. Check your findings with `answer-key.md` only after attempting the exercises.
6. Save `quality-check-pattern.txt` as a starting block, then adapt it to each task. A vague “check your work” is insufficient.

Everything here is self-authored fictional training material. No private files, real registration, image licences, sending, purchasing or publishing is involved. The examples are not claims about an external assistant's observed output. No specific vendor, account or paid plan is needed.

## Completion criteria
Your three templates name the evidence, details to inspect and observable decision rule; preserve original meaning and conditions; expose missing information; and keep external approval separate. Inspect actual final/exported output rather than relying only on the editor. If evidence or permission is unavailable, revise, stop or use the manual fallback.
