# Optional private voice-practice workspace

Open `Academy-Voice-Practice.html` in a current browser after extracting the whole folder. Keep the five WAV files beside it. The checked phrase and English meaning are in `checked-phrases.csv`. The original eight practice documents remain unchanged.

This self-created Academy tool is a real manual fallback, not ChatGPT, Google Translate, Android Recorder or a live AI voice assistant. It has working audio playback, an actual browser microphone recorder, local recording playback/delete, editable practice requests and private notes. Permit microphone access if your browser supports it; if access is unavailable, use your own recorder or type instead. No audio or notes are uploaded by this HTML file. Closing the tab discards its notes and recording. Ordinary microphone permission is still required; the course QA used a clearly identified synthetic microphone fixture, not a human recording.

## Listen, record, compare

1. Select the checked phrase and play `phrase.wav`: “Quisiera una barra de pan, por favor.” It means “I would like one loaf of bread, please.” Keep the quantity one and the request polite.
2. Listen to `barra.wav` and `phrase-slow.wav`, then record your own comfortable repetition in the Private recorder tab. Play it back. Choose one focus; stop if uncomfortable.
3. Use `repair.wav` for “¿Puede repetirlo más despacio, por favor?” and the authored fictional bakery-worker example `worker.wav` for “Buenos días. ¿Qué desea?” These are actual eSpeak NG Spanish synthetic examples, not a unique correct accent or human/AI conversation recording.
4. Record observations in your practice log, not a fluency score. Check important local wording with a capable speaker or qualified language professional.

## Read the evidence labels

The Conversation tab contains one actual retained model response to a typed practice request. It is a replay, not a live assistant, and no human speech was supplied. The transcript tab shows the actual Qwen3-ASR observation of the supplied synthetic `phrase.wav`: “Quisiera una vaga de pan, por favor.” This differs from the intended “barra”; it is retained verbatim, not silently corrected or used to judge a learner. The app does not automatically transcribe new microphone recordings.

The “para de pan” mismatch, the quantity follow-up, positive encouragement and copyable requests are explicitly authored illustrative examples. They are not claimed as observed live model responses. The lesson’s conditional retry method does not require a recognizer to make a particular error or produce a matching second attempt.

Privacy: use only your own voice or material you have permission to use. Do not capture private conversations. Delete recordings you do not need. For a five-minute session, use one minute to prepare, two to converse with your available tool or practise manually, one to retry and one to note what helped.
