Practice kit for Practical AI, Lesson 72. All event records and the flawed answer are self-created fictional teaching materials. Credits are not real money. Nothing has been booked, sent or published.

## Start
Read Original_Request.md and 05_Generated_Answer.md. Keep the flawed answer unchanged as a comparison. ## Exercises
1. Explain why public logistics warrant full source and calculation checks. Separate each claim in Claim_Check_Blank.csv. Classify factual, numerical, interpretive or action claims.
2. Open each original evidence file, rather than trusting a citation label. Enter an exact filename and section/row for each consequential claim. Mark supported, contradicted, partly supported or unresolved; use STOP for the release decision.
3. Check the public programme against doors and setup times. Audit capacity and access without extending level-entry evidence into a claim about every part of the venue.
4. In Calc open 04_Budget.fods. Verify B2:B5 against the CSV, then enter =SUM(B2:B5) into B6. Inspect the range before accepting the result. Calculate the difference from 525 and a rough estimate. Do not treat a supplied formula or answer key as an observed application result.
5. Write a corrected draft with the age/adult conditions, precise access features, no quiet room and current planned cost. Keep the parking and volunteer contact/deadline questions visible. Do not invent answers or publication.
6. Export your own draft as Riverside_Reading_Day_REVIEW_REQUIRED.pdf. Open the PDF itself and check full page edges, 120, 575, all qualifications, all three questions and the DO NOT PUBLISH status for clipping. A completed reference text does not prove your exported PDF.
7. Submit the unchanged flawed answer, completed claim table, evidence locations, calculation, revised draft, inspected PDF, unresolved questions, decision and one limitation/improvement.

## Checks and manual fallback

## Pass criteria
Every important claim has a traceable source or an explicit unresolved label. Arithmetic and range are independently checked. The corrected artifact preserves all qualifications. Accept the audit record, revise the draft and STOP publication. This exercise requires no AI account and no external send/publish action.
