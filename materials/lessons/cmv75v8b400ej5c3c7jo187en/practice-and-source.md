# Self-authored basic mathematics source and fresh practice
Fictional teaching questions; try without hints before opening the key.

For addition of fractions, express both using a common denominator, add numerators and keep that denominator. Simplify if possible. For example 1/2+1/3=3/6+2/6=5/6. Adding numerators and denominators separately does not preserve the quantities.
A percentage is a quantity out of 100. Twenty percent of 50 means 20/100×50. A reduction amount and the final price are different questions. Align decimal place values when adding. A ratio can be simplified by dividing both parts by the same nonzero factor.
