# Audience and Main Message Brief — blank practice copy
Complete these five lines before choosing a template, color or layout.

Audience: [Who specifically will receive this? Avoid “everyone.”]
Desired understanding: [What should be clear after reading or viewing?]
Desired action: [What reasonable supported response should follow?]
Main message: [One complete sentence connecting the need and response.]
Verified facts and gaps: [Copy the source facts separately from unknowns.]

Optional supporting fields from the lesson:
Current situation: [What might the audience not yet know?]
Language guidance: [What vocabulary can you reasonably assume?]

Distinguish your communication decisions from facts in the source. Write “not confirmed” for registration; do not replace the gap with an instruction to register now.
