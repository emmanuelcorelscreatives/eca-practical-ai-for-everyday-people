## One recognition mismatch

## Make an explanation usable
Explain the mouth movement in everyday words, and avoid technical symbols. Give one comfortable practice step. Do not assign an unsupported fluency score.

## Repair a conversation

## Bound the feedback
Focus on one category this turn: meaning, word choice, grammar, intelligibility, stress or pacing. Quote what you understood, identify one issue, and give one retry. After two or three tries, return to the conversation.
