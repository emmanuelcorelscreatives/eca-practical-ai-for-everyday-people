# Brief self-check and expected checks

These are planning checks for your brief, not evidence that an AI workflow passed.

- Beneficiary is one coordinator, not everyone. Problem names the difficulty of finding decisions/tasks in mixed notes.
- Trigger requires authorized notes. Required notes and date are present before starting; an absent owner/deadline stays Not stated.
- Output is one inspectable action list, with confirmed actions, proposals and questions separate.
- Criteria are observable: source references, no guessing, exact details, meaning, readability and named review. Looks good and saves time alone are insufficient.
- Time includes preparation, setup, checking, corrections, copying and maintenance. Cost includes subscription and usage, in an explicitly chosen currency and period. Fill personal numeric limits before beginning later work.
- Permission to read never silently expands into sending, publishing, deleting or shared-record changes.
- Complexity has no more than three simple stages, and allows manual transfer. No linked integrations are needed.
- Reviewer is the volunteer coordinator. Review fields remain blank until review occurs. Reviewer may accept, correct or stop; a generated draft is never source evidence.
- Manual fallback copies source-supported confirmed actions into the blank template, marks missing details and reviews every line; retain separate proposals/questions. Conflicts block acceptance.
- Planned cases cover normal notes, missing owner/date and conflicting notes. No result or score is claimed. Lesson 236 prepares the prompt/input pack; this lesson ends with a bounded brief.

Optional hand-copy answer: Inspect the loose gate latch; Mina; 2026-11-09; Confirmed action; N1. Check all fields against N1. N2 has Not stated for owner and due date. N3 is proposal only; N4 is unanswered. The unresolved amendment is not permission to replace N1 with a new deadline.

If your brief says manage everything, remove recording, messaging, calendars and shared-record changes. Keep supplied notes → draft action list → coordinator review. Save your own narrower brief.
