Ordered list: **2, 3, 3, 4, 18**.

2 + 3 + 3 + 4 + 18 = 30. There are five observations.

Mean = 30 ÷ 5 = **6**. Median = the third ordered value = **3**.

18 is larger than the other values and raises the mean. The median remains in the central group. Successful independent work must include both calculations and an explanation of the difference, rather than only recalling the two numbers.
