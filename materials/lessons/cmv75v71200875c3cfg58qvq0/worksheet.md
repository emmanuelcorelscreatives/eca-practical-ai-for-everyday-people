# Open the Source and Find the Evidence

Fictional Academy exercise for Lesson 51. Open `index.html` directly in a browser. No installation, account, private data, or network is required. All garden details and the answer fixture are self-authored teaching material, not real-world claims or a live AI response.

1. Split the answer into hours, tours, and packs claims. Copy each exact claim.
2. Open its citation. Confirm the actual page title and identity.
3. Use Find: Control-F on Windows/Linux/ChromeOS or Command-F on Mac. Search `9 a.m.`, `guided tour`, then `activity pack`, `printed`, and `free`.
4. Read the heading, full sentence, nearby sentences, and limiting notes. A highlighted phrase alone proves nothing.
5. Record the cited link, passage/location, one of the four decisions, and correction or unresolved note in `worksheet.html` or `worksheet.csv`.
6. For packs, first record the unhelpful homepage route. Then open `search.html`, search `printed activity packs`, and follow Family Activities. Preserve your original note when updating the decision.
7. Check the workshop sentence and retain every qualifier. Finish with the checks below before reading `answers.md`.

Supported: the complete claim is established. Partly supported: a narrower part is established. Not supported: the opened passage is irrelevant, contradictory, or too limited for the claim. Cannot check: the available route does not let you access or locate evidence. This does not prove the claim false.

## Checks

- Hours: identify the exceptional day and its exact hours.
- Tours: name all four limits in the passage. Does a shared phrase prove “always”?
- Packs: explain why the first result is Cannot check, then how the manually opened passage changes the decision.
- Workshops: classify “Some workshops may be available with advance booking,” then explain why replacing “may” with “will” changes the claim.
- Keep the original citation distinct from manually found evidence. Does each correction preserve uncertainty and quantity?
