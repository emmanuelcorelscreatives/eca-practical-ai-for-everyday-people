# Lesson 87 — Practise Before Revealing the Answer
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 15

Aim: attempt one question independently, request only the smallest useful hint, revise from method feedback, and finish a fresh question without help. No paid study feature is required. These are Academy-authored, fictional low-stakes practice examples, not claims about a particular assistant's live output.

Start with `worksheet.md`, keep `answer-guide.md` closed, and use `prompts.txt` in any general assistant you are permitted to use. Or open `practice.html` locally in a browser: questions are visible and hints/answers begin collapsed. The page sends nothing to a server; refresh discards typed work. Save your notes yourself before closing. Keyboard users can Tab to a disclosure and press Enter or Space. A screen reader announces expanded/collapsed state. Someone who can read an answer key can prepare separate hints for the paper fallback.

Files:
- `worksheet.md`: learner attempts, hint log, revisions, fresh unaided attempt.
- `source-garden.txt`: self-authored fictional paragraph; use only its stated facts.
- `prompts.txt`: exact practice constraints and source-only instruction.
- `answer-guide.md`: separate worked answers and source checking rubric; open only after attempts.
- `practice.html`: optional offline practice page with progressive disclosures.
- `checks.json` and `validate.py`: machine-readable arithmetic/source and page checks; optional developer validation, not required learner software.
- `LICENSE.txt`: permission to copy, adapt and use these self-authored examples.

Suggested 15-minute practice: 2 minutes set topic/level/one-question rule; 4 minutes first attempt and at most one hint at a time; 3 minutes revision and calculator check; 3 minutes fresh unaided question; 3 minutes paragraph/source verification. These are flexible practice timings, not a change to the recorded lesson runtime.

For any assistant: do not upload private or unlicensed material. A generated hint may leak an answer; stop reading it, return to your attempt, and strengthen the next instruction. An assistant's agreement is not independent proof. Verify arithmetic with a calculator and reading claims against the supplied paragraph. No factual claim about a named application's current interface is required by this kit.
