# Situation, Task, Action, Result
Communication aid, not a guarantee of interview success.

| Part | Fictional source example | Your evidence / missing detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation | Customer confused about delayed order | |
| Task | Responsibility not fully recorded | Confirm your responsibility; do not assume |
| Action | Checked information, explained next step simply, confirmed needs | Specify only your own actions |
| Result | General statement: customer understood process | Exact result not recorded; confirm what changed |

If a result is unknown, say **result not recorded**. Distinguish team work from personal work. Every achievement and qualification must be supportable.
