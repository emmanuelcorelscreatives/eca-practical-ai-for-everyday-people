# Percentage Basics
Academy-authored permission-cleared study note · Lesson 86

## Definition
Percentage means an amount per hundred. To find p percent of an amount x, divide p by 100 and multiply by x: (p / 100) × x. The original quantity need not contain exactly one hundred items.

## Representation and limits
A board of 100 equal tiles is a useful representation of a per-hundred scale. Selected tiles represent the selected proportion. It does not mean every real group contains 100 objects. Percentages also apply to continuous measurements such as litres; decimal percentages do not require whole tiles. A percentage may exceed 100 when the comparison permits a larger amount than its reference whole, for example 150 is 150% of 100.

## Worked example
25% of 80 = (25 / 100) × 80 = 0.25 × 80 = 20. Independently, 25% is one quarter, and 80 / 4 = 20.

## Check the model, not just the picture
Keep the equal-part, selected-part and per-hundred relationships. Revise any wording that suggests all quantities must have 100 countable pieces. If an analogy's central relationship conflicts with the concept, discard it and return to the definition.
