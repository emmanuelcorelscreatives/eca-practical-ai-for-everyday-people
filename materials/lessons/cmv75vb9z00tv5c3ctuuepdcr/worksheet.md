# Lesson 181 — Map Input, Processing, Review and Output
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

## What you will make
A four-stage map and a checked one-page organizer review copy for the **fictional Riverside Skills Workshop**. These self-created materials are permission-cleared practice data; they are not a real event or observed AI output. No account connection, publication, message, purchase or registration is needed.

1. Open `approved-input.md`. Name the input and its permission boundary. Ignore the separate brainstorming note; preserve both missing fields.
2. Open `processing-instructions.txt`. You may give those instructions and the approved input to any available assistant. Label its response **Generated draft — not verified**. Interfaces and account features vary. No assistant is necessary: copy each supplied fact into the document headings yourself.
3. For an immediate exercise, use `practice-draft.md`. It is deliberately self-authored flawed practice material, not a captured assistant response. Find every changed or unsupported field.
4. Open `field-review.csv` in a spreadsheet or a text editor. Compare each field against the approved input. Review the date including the year, time, location, topic, audience, capacity, contact and accessibility independently. Do not infer correctness from other matching rows.
5. Correct the draft using supported information only. Keep contact and accessibility visibly missing. Compare your result with `checked-review-copy.md` and `answer-key.md` after doing the exercise.
6. Complete `workflow-template.md`. Label the first arrow “Approved notes and instructions”, the next “Generated draft — not verified”, and the final “Checked information only”. Include a concrete start and observable finish.
7. Open your actual final document and inspect headings, wrapping, clipped text, all facts and both missing labels. A correct chat response alone does not prove the destination artifact is correct.

## Finish condition
The organizer has a readable, checked one-page **review copy**, with unsupported details excluded and unresolved contact/accessibility labels visible. In this offline exercise, save the file and have a practice partner inspect it; do not pretend it was sent. Confirmation of the missing details is outside this exercise.

## Keep it simple
Remove repeated conversions or extra tools that change or check nothing. A second generated answer is not independent evidence. The source notes are the review evidence. The manual fallback performs the same organization and field-by-field comparison without any assistant.
