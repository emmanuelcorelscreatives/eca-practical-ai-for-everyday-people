Access recommendation

VIEWER PAGE 3
Reading the recommendation
This note does not state that the garden is open every day. Do not replace a scheduled-hours qualification with a broader statement. It does not specify a daily timetable.

Evidence and interpretation
A quotation reproduces document wording. An interpretation is the reader's explanation. A nearby citation is a route to evidence, not proof that every word of a generated claim is supported.
