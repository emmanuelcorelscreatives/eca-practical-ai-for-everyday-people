# Lesson 137 — Verify Before Booking or Buying
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

All names, properties, dates, prices and policies here are a **fictional training scenario**, never live inventory or an offer. No payment, identity documents, real account or completed booking is required. All sample content is Academy-authored and permission-cleared.

## Use the kit
1. Read `scenario.json` and the original brief. Open `harborlight-training.html` locally in your browser. Start at AI lead (which shows the original brief), then Provider → Dates → Room → Accessibility → Rates → Conditions → Price. Select or remove optional breakfast yourself and open Final review; the choice and selected total persist during this open app session. Keep the AI lead separate from checked evidence.
2. Open `Final-verification.fods` in real LibreOffice Calc. The mandatory cost formula is C11 `=SUM(C7:C10)`, selected total is C13 `=C11+C12`, remaining budget C15 `=C14-C13`. Change C12 from 36 to 0 to remove optional breakfast, then restore 36.
3. Complete `verification-blank.csv` from the simulated provider evidence, not the AI draft. Compare with `verification-worked.csv` only after your attempt.
4. Work through `exercises.md` and check `expected-results.md`.

This kit teaches a verification method, not consumer, immigration or financial advice. For an actual decision, independently open current provider sources and relevant official entry requirements/consumer guidance. Training dates and prices must never be reused as present-day facts.

The worked Checked at timestamp is an explicitly illustrative training inspection time, not a real provider check. When practising, replace it with your own actual local-app inspection time, and for real transactions record when you opened the actual provider source. The original unverified AI claim said **with breakfast and free cancellation**; the provider evidence changes this to an optional EUR 36 breakfast and deadline-limited Flexible cancellation.

## Final control
The essential door-width measurement is unknown. If required, PAUSE and contact the provider through its independently verified published channel. Do not accept terms, select paid extras, enter payment details or confirm a booking on another person's behalf. A failed page or stale screenshot does not establish availability.
