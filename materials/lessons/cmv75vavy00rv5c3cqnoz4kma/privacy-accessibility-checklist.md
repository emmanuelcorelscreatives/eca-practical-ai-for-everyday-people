# Before you speak
- Only fictional/permission-cleared practice content; never private identifiers or bystander conversations.
- Confirm actual keyboard/app language, microphone permission and supported features.
- Choose a quiet safe place, normal steady speech; stop if unreliable.
- Names, numbers, dates and unclear words checked against the source.
- Manual correction followed by a full reread.
- Suitable typing or other accessible fallback; voice is optional.
- Confirm recipient and external facts separately; do not send this exercise.

Four-step routine: **Speak → Inspect → Correct → Use**. Higher-stakes money, safety, rights or commitments need more careful checking and appropriate independent confirmation.
