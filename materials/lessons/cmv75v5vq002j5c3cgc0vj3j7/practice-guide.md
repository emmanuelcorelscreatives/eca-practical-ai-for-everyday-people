# Private event clip practice
## Inputs and boundaries
Use only the supplied abstract `self_created_banner.jpg` and a recording you make yourself from `recording-script.txt`. The announcement is fictional. No event has been scheduled or advertised. The script includes no person's name, real date, address or organization. Do not imitate another person's voice. Participation in recording is optional.

Create a local folder `Event Clip Practice`. Keep originals separate from edited output; after the initial three-file input check, create `Originals` and `Edited Output` subfolders. Preserve the original image and audio in `Originals` and work with copies. Do not share either folder.

## Permission gate
Open the checklist before importing media into an editing tool. For the supplied banner, write Academy self-authored geometric banner and licence CC BY 4.0. For your newly recorded audio, identify yourself as the creator and only speaker only after listening. If a bystander is heard, do not claim Creator only: remove that input, record again privately, or use the text-only fallback. Record purpose as 20-second fictional event announcement and audience as Private course practice. The nominal script length is a practice target, not a measured duration; use your actual recording's length.

Separate Capture, Edit, Share and Synthetic identity. For this exercise basic cleanup is allowed, cloning is not authorized and public sharing is not approved. Record your own deletion plan after practice. The Creator-only example's change/withdrawal field is Not applicable: creator's own material; this is not a rule for another person's participation. Review licence conditions for the banner separately from rights in your own recording. If any gate question is uncertain, stop that input and choose a fallback.

## Allowed instruction
“Create a private practice clip. Use self_created_banner.jpg and my_own_narration.wav. Do not clone or alter identity. Add accurate captions. Do not publish.”

Use a local media editor you already have, if available. Import only the checked banner and your audio. Keep original narration selected; a licensed neutral synthetic narrator is an optional alternative only when that actual voice's licence permits the intended use. No synthetic recording is supplied or represented as your original voice in this kit. A tool that uploads media needs a separate permission, storage and access check before import.

Compare captions to your recording word by word, especially names, numbers and negative words. Inspect the image for faces, reflections, addresses and badges. Listen for background voices, cuts that change meaning and altered emphasis. Mark the result as Generated or edited output — private practice. Disclosure does not replace missing permission.

## Export and review
If your editor supports local export, save `practice_event_clip.mp4` under `Edited Output`; keep sharing/public-link controls off. Confirm actual folder/account access instead of treating a filename as proof of privacy. Do not create or test a public link. Open the saved output and inspect captions, image, audio, metadata, audience and access. Keep original and edited files distinct. Do not claim export succeeded until you have actually opened the file. Delete practice recordings according to your recorded plan; keep a minimal non-sensitive checklist if useful.

## Manual fallback
If recording, permission or tool access is uncertain, type the announcement text and use the supplied abstract graphic without audio. Do not invent a cleared recording or a completed export. Complete the same permission decision and record “No audio; text-only fallback; no publication.” An uncertain image can be replaced by a new abstract graphic you draw yourself. An uncertain voice can be replaced by your own consensual recording or an appropriately licensed neutral option after checking its conditions.

## Expected results
The banner inspection finds no identifiable person/address/logo. Synthetic identity remains No cloned identity. Audience is Private course practice; public sharing stays NOT APPROVED. The completed checklist describes your actual inputs and access, never a copied claim. A new audience or purpose pauses the workflow for a fresh decision. The final artifact, if made, is an edited practice output, not original evidence.
