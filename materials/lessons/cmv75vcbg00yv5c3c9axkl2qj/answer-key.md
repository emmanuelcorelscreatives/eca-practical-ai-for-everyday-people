# Worked reasoning and checks
These are choices for the fictional exercise, not universal suitability scores.

The workshop enquiry candidate recurs several times weekly, has the explicit new-response trigger and stable fields, produces one inspectable internal item, can wait for review, and has a manual copy-and-compare route. Receipt images can vary in layout, legibility, currency and date format; inbox replies involve varied language and an external action. Those concerns make the alternatives harder first projects here, not impossible tasks forever. Do not pretend all factors carry equal weights.

## Input classification
- WQ-001: complete structured record; prepare an internal Needs review item. Preferred date remains 2026-12-03, not a booking.
- WQ-002: ordinary structured input; blank optional Notes is valid. Do not invent notes.
- WQ-003: Preferred date missing; stop for human attention rather than invent a date.
- WQ-004: Notes says the request is unclear; route to human attention even though required fields are present. A syntactic field check does not resolve meaning.
- WQ-005: Advanced engine rebuilding is outside the exercise's stated workshop scope; human attention, no approval.
- WQ-006: complete structured record; keep the contact method Email (practice only), with no address or message send.

## Deliberately faulty copies
`review-item-checks.csv` WQ-001 has Preferred date 2026-12-04: it must be 2026-12-03. WQ-006 is incorrectly Approved: the lesson's result must remain Needs review. These rows are intentional checking fixtures; they do not represent an executed automation.

## WQ-001 manual fallback
Request ID WQ-001; Name or group Fictional Cedar Group; Workshop topic Everyday spreadsheets; Preferred date 2026-12-03; Contact method Phone (practice only); Notes Morning preferred; Status Needs review. A person decides clarity, scope and next action. Do not confirm a booking or contact anyone.

Minimum planned permissions: read submitted fields and create internal review items. Sending messages, publishing, purchasing and deleting are excluded. Actual connector permissions vary; confirm before implementation. AI is optional: direct field copying may be sufficient. If connections fail, the same manual template and checks remain useful. Stable fields and an available reviewer are assumptions to confirm, not observed facts about a live organization.
