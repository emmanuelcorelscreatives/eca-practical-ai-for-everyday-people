# Recover from a Missing or Poor Input
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Lesson 186

All workshop records in this kit are fictional Academy teaching fixtures, not a real event or an actual assistant response. They match the lesson's Riverside Skills Workshop example. No private contact data is present. A generated draft is not verified evidence.

## Run the recovery
1. Open `source-brief.md`. Keep its original Date blank. Do not open a final editor yet. The event cannot be announced without a date, including year.
2. Paste the source and `assistant-prompt.txt` into an approved assistant, or follow the manual route below. Inspect what actually arrived: file label alone does not prove pages, tables or contents are readable. Do not claim the static expected response is an actual live run.
3. Record STATUS: NEEDS CLARIFICATION. Ask “What is the confirmed event date, including the year?” Contact permission was not supplied: omit contacts and ask before publishing them. See `handoff-before.csv`.
4. In this fictional exercise, now open `clarification.md`. Preserve its origin and restriction beside the original brief; do not rewrite the historical blank as though the date was always known.
5. Resume with the original source plus clarification, explicitly forbidding a contact line. Label any generated draft READY FOR CHECK, never verified.
6. Compare every event field with opened evidence. Import `handoff-after.csv` into a spreadsheet if useful, but independently check its rows. Date must be 18 November 2026; time 10:00–12:00; venue Community Room B; capacity 24; audience adult beginners in smartphone photography. No contact line.
7. Only after all checks mark READY FOR EDITOR. Build a slide in your approved editor using `checked-slide-content.md`. Open the finished slide itself and compare every field: correct checking table does not prove a correct exported artifact. Do not publish or send this fictional event.

## Manual route
If the assistant, connection or integration is unavailable, read the brief, mark Date required, and send the precise question through an approved channel for a real task. For this fictional practice use the supplied clarification, record it beside the brief, and complete an ordinary editor draft marked NOT FINAL until checked. There is no need to upload personal information or buy any product.

## Three additional failures
A. `conflicting-note.md` conflicts with the clarification: do not choose a date by order, recency or repetition. Expose the conflict and ask the authorized source which is correct.
B. Treat “Community Room 8?” as an uncertain OCR observation from a blurred image, not a corrected venue. Request a clearer original or ask its supplier. No real blurred image or OCR run is claimed by this text exercise.
C. A purported workshop-brief.pdf attachment cannot be opened. State exactly which content is unavailable; do not claim the attachment label proves its pages were read. No broken attachment file is distributed here; simulate this state by withholding the source from your partner.

For each, fill `recovery-log.csv` with affected field, problem, focused question or safe fallback, status and source reference. Stop before final output while required evidence is unresolved. A low-impact private color preference may use a labelled assumption; a recipient, deadline, price, permission or safety detail needs a stronger stop and independent review.

## Expected checks
Initial date absent -> STOP. “Friday” alone -> incomplete. Opposing 18/19 November dates -> conflict, no winner. Unreadable venue -> request evidence, no silent Room B/8 substitution. Missing contact permission -> omit and ask. Confirmed no-contact restriction -> omit even after recovery. Genuine live assistant wording may differ; assess behavior and evidence rather than exact prose.
