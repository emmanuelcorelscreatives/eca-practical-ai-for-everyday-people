# Source-checking worksheet
Goal: label individual claims Supported, Unsupported or Unresolved; retain only evidence-backed wording.

1. Read draft-and-prompt.md. Copy the four claim groups into claims-blank.csv or draw six columns on paper: Claim, Importance, Opened evidence, Status, Correction, Next action. Record “No source opened yet” until you inspect a passage.
2. Base case: open organizer.md, seeds.md and access.md. Record each source filename, its updated date, event/year/place scope and exact supporting or limiting passage. Classify each claim as written. Split compound claims when necessary. State how its possible harm affects checking priority.
3. Write a repaired notice using only these three sources. Trace every sentence to a worksheet row. Keep the supply condition and greenhouse limitation; do not infer a seed quantity or universal access.
4. Separate conflict case: now open conflict-calendar.md. Add a new date row showing both passages. Reassess the date without erasing the base-case record. Write a conflict-case notice and a next action; no organizer contact has occurred in this exercise.
5. Check your work with answer-key.md. Explain why missing support is not proof of falsehood and why two conflicting relevant sources are not settled by an AI confidence score.

Definitions: Supported = evidence directly covers the exact claim and scope. Unsupported = adequate support was not established (not automatically false). Unresolved = credible evidence is incomplete, ambiguous or conflicting. “Partly supported” describes a sentence needing splitting/narrowing; the three status labels remain the choices.

Manual fallback: use these local source files and a paper table. In real work, an ordinary browser can open the underlying page; find the passage, check title/date/place/year, record limitations. A search snippet alone is insufficient. If a consequential detail cannot be settled, show uncertainty or omit it and seek authoritative clarification.

Final checklist: every claim has a source passage or explicit uncertainty; no unsupported numbers; no removed conditions; no broader access promise; no claim of contacting anyone; base and conflict cases remain separate.
