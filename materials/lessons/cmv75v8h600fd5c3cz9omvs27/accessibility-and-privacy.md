# Manual route and privacy
If live conversation is unavailable, use supported text-to-speech or translation playback. Select the correct languages, enter the checked phrase, and listen. Make one private repetition in your device recorder and listen back. Compare intended words, obvious pauses, prominent stress and comfortable pace. One synthetic model is not the only acceptable accent. A recording comparison does not certify fluency.

Type instead of speaking, repeat at your own pace, or stop. Use headphones only at a comfortable volume and a place where listening is safe. Follow the platform's current permission and deletion controls; exact labels vary. Use your own voice or explicit permission-cleared material, fictional scenarios and no unnecessary personal details. Remove practice recordings you do not need, considering any synced/cloud copies separately. Do not record or upload private conversations belonging to others.

A capable speaker can check local vocabulary and important meaning. A qualified language professional should review wording with serious consequences. This practice pack provides no medical, legal or language certification.
