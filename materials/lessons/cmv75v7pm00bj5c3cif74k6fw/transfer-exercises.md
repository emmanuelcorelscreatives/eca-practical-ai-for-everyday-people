# Transfer practice

All scenarios are fictional. For each, record intended use, must-pass checks, one decision, reason/evidence, next permitted action and fallback. Do not add facts.

A. A colleague needs only an independently checked calculation of the listed costs for an internal arithmetic exercise. Your readable note correctly shows 165, source inputs and units, and prominently states that capacity and additional fees are unresolved and the note does not authorize spending or suitability. You have checked every condition for this limited use. Accept, revise or stop?

B. Final spending/suitability approval is required today, but the reliable authorized source for capacity and fees cannot be reached and you cannot verify those facts in time. Can a guessed “probably fine” close the gap? What route must stop, and what can you retain as fallback?

C. The revised document correctly shows both gaps, but after exporting, the warning and fee-status line are clipped off the final file. Which artifact governs the decision and what must be rechecked?

D. Your spreadsheet formula is syntactically correct, but you copied 8 hours rather than the source's 6. Is a correct multiplication result enough? Name the failed check without guessing another input.
