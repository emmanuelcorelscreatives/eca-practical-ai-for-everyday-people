# Compare Independent Evidence
Goal: count separate evidence paths for a precise claim, rather than counting pages.

Practice claim: “The Riverside Learning Fair was moved to 18 May 2027 at Harbor Hall.” Separate the new scheduled date and venue from the historical assertion “was moved.”

1. Open index.html, then each of the four publications. Use your browser’s page search for “18 May 2027.” Read the surrounding paragraph and attribution. A snippet or confident summary is only a lead.
2. Fill source-checking-blank.csv (or copy its eight headings onto paper). For each page record the exact supporting passage, linked upstream source, family, independence, relevant date and scope, and result. Open every linked upstream document before accepting it. Write unknown when a chain ends unclearly.
3. Compare Town Update, Community News and City Bulletin. Do different names or wording create new evidence? Trace all three back to the notice.
4. Open Riverside Reporter’s calendar and interview links. Who maintains those records? Does the calendar support the claim directly? Do not count calendar, venue confirmation and reporter as three more independent families merely because they are three documents.
5. Draw two branches. One begins at the organizer notice and reaches three derivative pages. The other contains venue calendar/confirmation and reaches Riverside Reporter. Label these families.
6. Write a two-sentence conclusion for date/venue. Then separately assess whether the venue record proves the event was moved. State exactly what remains unsupported or unknown.
7. Challenge: imagine a fifth page repeating Town Update without a link. Record its origin as unclear until you can trace it; do not manufacture a new independent path. Imagine the calendar’s year instead reads 2026: would it support this edition?

Optional AI assistance: copy the supplied source text (all explicitly fictional) and use prompts.md. Keep the actual response separately as an unverified lead. Compare every proposed attribution with the open pages and correct it. Two agreeing AI tools can share the same upstream assertion.

Manual fallback: use the linked local pages as your search results, open each, find the date, and trace links yourself. No live search is needed for fictional material. For a real check, use ordinary search for exact phrases, inspect attribution such as “according to” or “data from,” and record inaccessible origins as unknown.

Expected completion check: four publication rows; three share Organizer statement; Riverside Reporter adds Venue record and reporting for date/venue; two meaningful paths for scheduled date/venue; move history has narrower support. Quality, directness, timeliness and consequences matter more than any universal required number of links. Check answers.md afterward.
