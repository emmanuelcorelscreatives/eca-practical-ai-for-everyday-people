# Opened evidence — self-authored study note

This is an Emmanuel Corels Academy teaching note, not a third-party publication and not an AI-generated citation.

**Mean:** sum divided by number of observations. Every observation contributes to the total, including unusually large or small values.

**Median:** middle ordered value when the count is odd. Sort all observations before locating the middle one. These exercises all contain five observations, so the median is the third ordered value.

**Interpretation:** An unusually large value can pull the mean farther than the median. The median can better describe the central cluster in the particular examples here. Neither measure is always best: the question, the distribution and what the values mean affect the useful choice.

**Assumption:** values are equally weighted. **Missing information:** what these numbers represent and why a summary is needed. Arithmetic can be checked without that information; the appropriate interpretation may depend on it.
