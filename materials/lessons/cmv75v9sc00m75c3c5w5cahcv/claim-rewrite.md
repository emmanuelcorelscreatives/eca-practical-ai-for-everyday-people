# Preserve source boundaries
Deliberately overstated AI example: “Best choice because it stays cold all day and never leaks.”
Problems: an undefined promotion became a performance promise; mixed personal reports became a universal guarantee; exact market and bag dimensions remain unverified.
Faithful rewrite: “The listing claims all-day cooling, but I found no test conditions. User reports about leakage are mixed.”
Capacity can separately be recorded as directly stated 500 mL. That does not confirm cooling or fit.

Directly stated means the opened source explicitly states the field, not an independent laboratory test. Defined but unverified fits a measurable specified claim lacking independent checking; do not apply it to undefined all-day wording. Recurring user report is a pattern worth investigating without a measured population rate. Unknown means evidence was not found, not that a claim is false.

Verified purchase adds transaction context only. A free-product disclosure adds relationship context; it neither proves truth nor makes an experience automatically false. Identical wording warns of possible dependence, not proven coordination. Rules vary by location: for real endorsement concerns consult the applicable trusted consumer authority's current guidance.
