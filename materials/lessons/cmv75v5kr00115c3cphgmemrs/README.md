# Chat, Search and Source-Based Answers
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 2, Lesson 8.

Practice choosing the information route for the fictional Community Garden Open Day: generated ideas, retrieved current information, and answers grounded in a supplied source. The fictional event is a training example, not a real invitation. No account, purchase or publication is required. Product names in the lesson are illustrative; availability and interfaces can change.

Start with LAB.md. Open Community_Garden_Open_Day_Brief.pdf or its identical text companion. Use prompts.md and decision-worksheet.csv. Compare your work with EXPECTED-CHECKS.md. Save checked observations in checked-notes.csv. Never put private information in the demonstration.

The lesson's fictional event date is Saturday, 7 November 2026; it remains fixed as an authored exercise. It is not the date of your live library search. Record the date and time you actually check an official page.
