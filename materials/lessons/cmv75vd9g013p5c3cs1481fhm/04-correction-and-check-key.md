## Arithmetic from the lesson's illustrative figures
Case one: 4 + 2 + 10 + 6 + 8 = 30 minutes. Baseline less case one: 52 − 30 = 22 minutes.
Three cases: 30 + 34 + 38 = 102 minutes. 102 ÷ 3 = 34 minutes average, including recovery in the third total.
52 − 34 = 18 minutes difference. These are example differences, not guaranteed savings. The lesson does not provide separate category breakdowns for cases two/three or the manual baseline; do not invent them. The case-one worked row has zero additional recovery/maintenance; your actual maintenance may differ. Do not calculate a meaningful performance average from your own worksheet until comparable totals and checked outcomes exist.

## Interpretation and decision
In the fictional story, three corrected final artifacts meet the criteria. Initial drafts were not all correct: three corrections, two corrections, and one completion stop. The small example does not establish general reliability, safe unattended use or permission to publish. Required-field checking is the next proposed improvement because the missing accessibility fact exposed a risk. Benefit is a hypothesis until a fresh retest. A second AI's agreement does not prove truth.
