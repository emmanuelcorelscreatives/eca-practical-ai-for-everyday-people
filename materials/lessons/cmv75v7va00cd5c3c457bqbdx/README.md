# Preserve Your Voice — Lesson 76 practice kit
Use a permitted writing sample to choose preferences, then draft from separate facts and check meaning and voice yourself. This is a fictional, self-authored Academy practice pack, not a record of a real event or an actual assistant conversation. No account, upload or AI purchase is necessary.

Start with worksheet.md. Keep owned-sample.txt, approved-preferences.md and source-notes.txt separate. prompts.md offers optional copyable instructions for a general assistant; assistant output will vary. Use any ordinary editor for the manual route. Compare your work with answers.md only after attempting the exercises. meaning-check.csv is a printable fact-by-fact record. flawed-draft.txt is intentionally inaccurate study material.

The lesson’s dated fictional book swap is retained for source fidelity; it is not an upcoming event. Do not send or publish the announcement. Organizer name and contact method are unknown. This kit cannot supply them. No files run code, make network requests or send messages.

Model answers are authored examples. They are neither observed product responses nor the only acceptable wording. All text in this pack may be copied, modified and redistributed for practice under CC0-1.0; no third-party writing is supplied. With your own sample, remove confidential details and confirm permission before sharing it. Having access alone does not establish permission.
