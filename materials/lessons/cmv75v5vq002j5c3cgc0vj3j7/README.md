# Consent for Recordings, Images and Voices
Emmanuel Corels Academy — Practical AI for Everyday People, Stage 3 Lesson 17.

This offline practice kit teaches the permission gate in the saved lesson. It contains a self-authored abstract banner, a permission-checklist template and fictional scenarios. It contains no photographs of people, recorded personal conversations, cloned voices or real signatures. No completed external permission or publication is claimed.

## Start here
1. Read `practice-guide.md` and `permission_checklist.md`.
2. Open `self_created_banner.jpg` and check the actual image, not just its filename. It contains only geometric shapes, no people, words, address, logo or organization.
3. Read `recording-script.txt`. If you freely choose to participate, record it yourself in a quiet place using your device's recorder. Listen for other speakers and identifying details. Export your recording as `my_own_narration.wav`; do not rename an incompatible format to WAV. Do not upload your recording to this public course repository.
4. Copy `permission_checklist.odt` into your private `Event Clip Practice` folder. LibreOffice Writer can open it; a Markdown equivalent is supplied for any text editor. Fill the fields honestly for your actual inputs. The demo's completed Creator-only example is a fictional example, not permission for your inputs.
5. Keep only the three intended inputs in the practice folder: `self_created_banner.jpg`, your `my_own_narration.wav` and your `permission_checklist.odt`. Keep this kit's other instructions elsewhere. Follow the private workflow; never publish the practice output.
6. Complete `scenarios.csv`, compare `answer-key.md`, then complete the final check.

You may use the supplied banner for this private practice. The Academy's self-authored teaching text/banner are offered under CC BY 4.0; see `LICENSE.md`. That licence does not grant rights over any real person or your added recordings. Do not upload other people's identifiable media without the specific permission and local/organizational checks described in the lesson. This checklist is an educational aid, not a legal release or individualized legal advice.

## Completion evidence
Keep locally: your filled permission gate, your own recording, a caption comparison, a private export review and decisions for all five scenarios. A learner may complete the manual fallback instead of recording or using a media tool. No account, paid subscription, cloned voice or public link is required.
