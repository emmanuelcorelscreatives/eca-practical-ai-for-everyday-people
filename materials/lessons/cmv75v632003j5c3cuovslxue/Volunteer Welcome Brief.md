# Volunteer Welcome Brief

Fictional Academy practice task, created for this lesson. No real event or personal data.

- Audience: new volunteers.
- Purpose: welcome them and explain first day steps.
- Length: 120–150 words.
- Tone: warm and direct.
- Format: short opening followed by exactly three bullet points.
- Preserve: arrive at 08:45; use the community hall side entrance; bring a reusable bottle; ask Sam about access support.
- Flag missing information instead of guessing. Street address, parking information, phone number and email address are unknown.
