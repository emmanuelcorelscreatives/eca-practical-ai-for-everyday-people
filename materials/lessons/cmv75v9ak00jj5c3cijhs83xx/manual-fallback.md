# Unweighted fallback and open decision
For each venue, record the supplied facts, advantages, disadvantages, constraints, unknowns and the tradeoff you prefer. Cite the exact fictional source bullet. Keep your preference separate from the source claim.

Worked example: River Room has the lowest supplied fictional hire quote (£180) and listed projector/screen. This does not establish suitability: required step-free access and accessible toilet are unconfirmed. Its qualification remains unresolved until evidence confirms both. Do not let a high equipment rating compensate for a required access condition.

Cedar satisfies the exercise's stated access confirmation. Market cannot qualify until required access is verified; its lower quote and listed sound system do not compensate for that missing evidence. Travel assumptions still need checking. One learner may prefer Cedar's estimated shorter journey (an assumption requiring verification), another may justify Market's listed sound system. State the reason and what information could change the choice; no universal winner is warranted.

Six checks: What evidence supports each entry? What does each score mean? Who chose the weights? Are unknowns visible? Is a requirement non-negotiable? Do plausible changes reverse the winner?

Equal 25% weights are a modeling choice. A criterion spanning scores 2–9 may change totals more than one spanning 7–8. Equal displayed weights alone do not imply equal effective influence.
