# Preparation prompts (self-authored examples)

Use only fictional or safely minimized information. These prompts are teaching inputs, not evidence of model execution. Open and inspect original sources yourself. Do not paste private records merely because this worksheet has a field.

## Shared instruction
Use only the supplied record. Separate supplied facts (cite record ID), assumptions (do not silently add them), missing information, consequential questions and matters requiring qualified judgment. Preserve exact dates, parties, location, warnings and conditional words. Do not treat generated wording as opened evidence. Do not invent a register check or completed contact. Leave unresolved matters unresolved.

## H
FICTIONAL HEALTH SCENARIO: An adult reports new symptoms. Do not diagnose or recommend treatment. Organize H1 into Supplied facts, Assumptions, Missing information, Possible urgency indicators not established by the record, and Questions for an appropriately authorized health professional. Do not determine whether it is safe to wait. For a possible emergency, stop AI discussion and use the appropriate local emergency service.

## L
FICTIONAL LEGAL SCENARIO: Extract L1's exact wording, printed date, named parties and location. Separate document text from interpretation. Do not determine rights, obligations, strategy, validity or a binding deadline. Flag jurisdiction, identity, replacement notices and response timing for authorized legal review.

## M
FICTIONAL MONEY SCENARIO: Organize M1 into Objectives, When money is needed, Need for access, Ability to bear loss, Unknowns, and Questions for regulated review. No product ranking or recommendation. Suitability and claimed authorization remain undetermined. The appropriate real-world register and independent contact route depend on location and scope.

## S
FICTIONAL SAFETY SCENARIO: Organize S1 into Stop conditions already recorded, Manufacturer instructions to locate, Workplace controls to obtain, Unknowns, and Questions for a trained safety specialist. Do not provide repair or isolation steps. Do not infer that equipment is de-energized or safe.
