# Self-authored practice drafts
These examples were written for this Academy exercise. They are not transcripts of an assistant response and do not prove that a model ran or that an event exists.

## Deliberately faulty draft excerpt
> Join our community event! Register online before Friday to reserve a place.

“Register online before Friday” is unsupported: the source supplies neither a registration process nor a deadline. Do not add them because they sound plausible. The excerpt also fails to provide the requested complete format and event facts.

## Example draft for review
```text
Community Swap Day
Local residents, bring useful items to our community swap day.
- Venue: Riverside Community Hall.
- When: Saturday, 14 November, 10:00–13:00.
- Bring clean books, toys, or kitchen items.
- No electrical items.
For contact or registration details: [DETAIL NEEDED].
```

This draft has one headline, one introductory sentence, exactly four bullet points and one closing action, and is below 100 words. All five supplied facts remain unchanged. The closing line marks unknown information; it is a **review placeholder**, not a contact instruction ready for publication. Obtain the missing fact or remove that closing instruction before sharing. Do not assume the source specifies a year, street address, fee, organizer or registration requirement.

## Diagnosing misses
| Problem | Part to clarify |
|---|---|
| Wrong purpose | Goal |
| Unsuitable language for the audience | Context |
| Invented details | Input or missing-information constraint |
| Awkward usable structure | Output format |

Changing wording randomly is less useful than identifying which requirement was absent or unclear. Remove irrelevant background; retain facts, boundaries and the checking method that change the result.
