# Fictional community garden — worked planning brief

Self-authored illustrative planning example. Not an executed workflow, approved output or test report.

Beneficiary: Volunteer coordinator.
Problem: Meeting decisions and proposed tasks are difficult to find in mixed notes.
Trigger: After an authorized meeting note file is available.
Inputs: Permission-cleared meeting notes and meeting date, including decisions, suggestions, questions, stated owners and stated deadlines. Exclude unnecessary personal details. Without the note file or meeting date, do not start. An unstated action owner/deadline is recorded as Not stated; it is not guessed.
Output: Reviewed action list with separate confirmed actions, proposals, questions and source references.
Success criteria:
- Every listed action is supported by the supplied notes and linked to its passage.
- No owner or deadline is guessed: use Not stated where absent.
- Confirmed actions, proposals and unanswered questions remain separate.
- Names, dates, quotations and task wording match the opened notes.
- The artifact has clear headings, readable contrast and meaningful labels on the coordinator's usual laptop and phone, with an appropriate alternative format when needed.
- The volunteer coordinator checks source support, exact details, categories and usability before accepting, correcting or stopping.
Time: Must fit the time available for setup, review and correction; also count preparation, copying and maintenance. Personal amount/period: [to be decided].
Cost: Use existing tools unless total ongoing cost is acceptable. Local currency, total amount and period: [to be decided]. This example is not ready for execution until learner-specific limits are set.
Permissions: Use authorized notes; draft only; no sending, publishing, deleting or shared-record edits.
Complexity: Maximum three simple stages — supplied notes, draft action list, coordinator review. Manual transfer allowed. Tools: available assistant, ordinary editor and manual checklist; no integrations needed.
Reviewer: Volunteer coordinator checks the opened notes against every row and decides accept, correct or stop. No review has happened in this example.
Manual fallback: Copy confirmed actions from the notes into the blank template, mark missing details and review line by line. Keep proposals and questions separate. Stop for conflicts and request clarification.
Planned cases: Complete notes; missing owner or date; conflicting statements. These are plans, not tests or results.
Final scope: Help one volunteer coordinator create a reviewed action list from authorized meeting notes, without guessing details or taking external actions.
