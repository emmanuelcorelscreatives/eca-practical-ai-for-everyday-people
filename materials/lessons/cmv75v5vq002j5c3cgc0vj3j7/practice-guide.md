# Private event clip practice

## Actual course demonstration and your choices
The revised demonstration uses self-authored Academy text/banner and actual eSpeak NG neutral synthetic narration. The source text is `recording-script.txt`; the two editing inputs are `self_created_banner.jpg` and `practice_narration.wav`. The voice is a software machine voice, not a recording of the instructor and not an imitation of a real person. No externally granted consent or human speaker identity is claimed.

The announcement is fictional; no event has been scheduled or advertised. The script has no real person’s name, date, address or organization. Its nominal 20-second target is not a measurement. Actual locally generated audio duration must be measured. The actual course fixture was approximately 16.723 seconds; your software version or settings may differ.

If you want to use a human recording instead, record the supplied text yourself only if you freely choose to participate. Listen for bystanders and identifying details. Save it as `my_own_narration.wav` only if it is truly a WAV file; do not rename an incompatible format. Keep that separate route and its permission record distinct from the supplied synthetic example. The optional Academy fixture app deliberately blocks the unsupplied human-original option. Use your own local editor for your separately cleared human recording, or choose the manual fallback.

Create a private `Event Clip Practice` working folder. Keep original inputs under `Originals` and edited outputs under `Edited Output`; preserve original bytes. Keep these instructions outside the editing project. Open the checklist before importing anything into a media tool.

## Actual checks
Inspect the actual banner pixels for faces, reflections, badges, addresses and incidental identifying detail. Play the actual audio; inspect unexpected voices, identifying content, emphasis and meaning. Compare every caption to the recording, particularly names, numbers and negative words. An automatic caption check or manual checkbox is not proof that you listened.

The optional app derives its waveform and duration from real decoded audio. Its caption export gate compares text with the known self-authored fixture script; it is not speech recognition and cannot verify an arbitrary human recording. You must still listen. A selected `Licensed neutral synthetic narrator` option is appropriate for this fixture; do not select or claim an original human recording that is not supplied.

## Private export and inspection
If making a clip, save `practice_event_clip.mp4` under `Edited Output`. Keep originals unchanged and separate. Do not create a public link or post the practice result. Confirm actual filesystem/account access; a filename or a label does not make a folder private. The optional Academy app has no publication/account-sharing function and records actual owner/file permissions, rather than inventing vendor sharing toggles.

Open the actual saved output and inspect captions, image, audio, identifying metadata, audience and access. Mark it `Generated or edited output — private practice`. A constructed clip is not original documentary evidence. A successful export is not permission or proof of authenticity. In the course test the Chromium build lacked H264 playback: a real WebM preview was transcoded FROM the saved MP4 and played, and the MP4 was separately probed and fully decoded. The app labels this distinction; never call the preview direct MP4 browser playback.

Delete private working recordings/outputs according to the recorded plan. Keep a minimal nonsensitive decision record only if useful. Do not upload private generated files, recordings or output folders to the public kit repository.

## Manual fallback
If synthesis, recording, software, permissions or access is uncertain, type the announcement and use the abstract graphic without audio. Complete the gate and record `No audio; text-only fallback; no publication`. Do not invent a cleared recording, a permission grant, a model result or a completed export. An uncertain image can be replaced with your own abstract graphic. An uncertain voice can be replaced with your freely chosen recording or an appropriately licensed neutral voice only after checking the relevant conditions.

## Expected checks
Synthetic identity is `No cloned identity`; audience is `Private course practice`; public sharing is `NOT APPROVED`. The checklist describes actual evidence, not copied assumptions. A changed use pauses the workflow. Disclosure, watermarks, metadata, provenance signals and detectors never replace missing permission or prove truth on their own.

The exact-fixture app verifies cryptographic hashes of the selected image and audio against the actual synthesis receipt and on-disk source before export. A substituted image or WAV is blocked; this is not a general editor for arbitrary media. Each new MP4 export runs and records its own checked full decode before success is reported.
