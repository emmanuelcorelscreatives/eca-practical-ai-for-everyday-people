# Fictional community planning brief
The community planning group must decide whether to plan a two-hour outdoor gathering at fictional Riverside Commons on November 14 or 15, 2026. It needs a decision-supporting research record by November 1, 2026. The decision options are an outdoor plan, an indoor alternative, or postponement.

The research includes conditions affecting a two-hour outdoor event, basic accessibility, and rules affecting use. Catering, performers/entertainment, fundraising, decorations and publicity are outside this decision's current scope.

Riverside Commons is invented. No actual address, accessibility audit, booking availability, weather forecast or permission is supplied. These are unresolved until an appropriate real venue and current evidence are chosen. November 1 is the initial decision-support deadline; changing conditions need rechecking near the actual event. Do not imply that a long-range forecast establishes event-day safety.
