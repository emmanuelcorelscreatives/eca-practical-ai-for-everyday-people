# Audience and Main Message Brief — worked example
HUMAN-AUTHORED PLANNING EXAMPLE. Audience and goal choices below are not claims about a surveyed population.

Audience: Local adults who have one small household item needing a simple repair and can attend the event.

Current situation: They may not know the event exists or whether their item is suitable.

Desired understanding: A free repair event is available at Riverside Library Hall on 14 November 2026, from 10:00 to 13:00, for one small household item.

Desired action: Decide whether to attend and prepare one suitable item. Check the registration method when confirmed.

Main message: Bring one small household item to the free Community Repair Event at Riverside Library Hall on Saturday, 14 November 2026, from 10:00 to 13:00.

Language guidance: Plain everyday English. Explain any unfamiliar repair terms. Do not assume technical knowledge.

Verified facts: Saturday, 14 November 2026; 10:00–13:00; Riverside Library Hall; free entry; bring one small household item.

Missing information: Registration method: not confirmed. A supported decision to prepare an item is possible, but an instruction to register immediately is not supported. The main message does not guarantee successful repair or acceptance of every item.
