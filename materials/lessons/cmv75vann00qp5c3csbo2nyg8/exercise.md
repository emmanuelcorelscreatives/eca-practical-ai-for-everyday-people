# Practice and manual fallback

## A. Classify sources

Create `Riverside_Repair_Day/01_originals`, `02_edits` and `03_final`. Copy the supplied pattern into originals. Record three categories separately: self-created, permission-cleared and unknown. Do not download a stranger's image merely to fill a category. Explain why attribution alone is insufficient.

## B. Make and inspect a design

Generate variations with the supplied brief if you have an appropriate tool and permission. Save the actual output and record its actual origin. Alternatively, create a manual square design using the supplied pattern and editable title/date/location. Label that version as a manual practice design. The manual fallback does not demonstrate a generated crowd; it is the lesson's explicitly allowed fallback.

Add the exact event details and the origin label appropriate to your method. Inspect the exported file for spelling, misleading realism, geometry, logos and accidental changes. Preserve the unedited output separately.

## C. Check OCR without inventing permissions

Open `ocr-source-card.txt`. Create your own image of those exact words or use your camera to scan your own copy. Keep the original image. Compare actual OCR output line by line. Leave an unreadable field empty rather than filling it from context. Correct only characters you can see, then mark the comparison manually checked. The source card's terms apply only to the supplied Academy pattern, not an arbitrary online image.

## D. Prepare a handoff

Include original assets, edited version, final export, source-and-permission record and completed checklist. Write one sentence about what was checked and one about what remains unknown. Keep Publish unchecked; this is fictional training material. If permission cannot be established, choose another source or the manual fallback.
