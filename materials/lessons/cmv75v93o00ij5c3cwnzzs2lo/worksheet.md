# Lesson 113 — Turn Voice Notes into Organized Material
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

Practice organizing authorized voice notes without turning possibilities into promises. This kit uses a self-authored fictional community-room event. No real event, volunteer commitment, recording or software result is represented.

## Three distinct layers
1. **Audio:** record yourself reading `recording-script.txt`, or have a consenting partner read it. Keep the original recording. The sample is short, not a claimed seven-minute recording. No audio file is supplied.
2. **Transcript:** use your available transcription tool, or replay short sections and type a manual transcript. Google Recorder on a supported Pixel is the lesson example, not a requirement. Device, language and transcription access vary. Do not upload confidential or third-party audio without authority.
3. **Organization:** use `organization-prompt.txt` with your corrected transcript, or sort the notes manually. Keep this separate from the transcript and label it generated organization until reviewed.

## Work through the exercise
- Choose the purpose “prepare event materials” before recording; reduce background noise and use a plain title such as “Community room event note”.
- Open `transcript-with-errors.txt`. It is a deliberately authored practice fixture, **not real ASR output**. Compare it against your recording and the reference script. Correct the name and quantity only; do not complete the plan. If your recording differs, use what was actually spoken rather than silently forcing the fixture.
- Preserve the uncertain date. If audio is unclear, mark it unclear, not confirmed.
- Make categories, a checklist or a draft using only the corrected source. Mark missing owners/deadlines as missing. A stated need is not an assigned commitment.
- Compare every output line with the corrected transcript and important details with your audio. Use `review-sheet.csv` to record evidence and decisions.
- Test the focused revision in `organization-prompt.txt`; inspect unchanged lines too.
- Transfer only the reviewed checklist into a new practice note/document. Keep possible ideas and unresolved items visibly distinct. Do not send anything or assign real people.
- After your own attempt, consult `answer-guide.md`.

## Completion check
The reviewed output preserves Samira, twenty-five sheets, the uncertain Thursday eighteenth, the possible welcome sign and the missing chair owner. It invents no date confirmation, owner, deadline or sent-message status. Every substantive item has a source; water remains an unassigned need.

No installation, paid account, private data or programming is required. The answer guide is an instructor-authored reference, not evidence that an assistant or recording app produced the result.
