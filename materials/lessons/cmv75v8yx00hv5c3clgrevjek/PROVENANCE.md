# Provenance and reuse

Self-authored Emmanuel Corels Academy practice materials for Lesson 109, “Capture Audio with Permission and Purpose”. The fictional planning script matches the lesson’s authored example. No real meeting, private account, third-party audio, device screenshot or observed application result is included. Learners supply their own permission-safe practice recording; no voice model output is represented as a human recording.

These are educational exercises, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice or consent certification. Apply relevant rules and stricter institutional policies. No software installation, account access, paid subscription or external upload is needed for the notes fallback.
