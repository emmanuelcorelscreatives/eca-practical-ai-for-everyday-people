# Define the Research Question — lesson 97
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

Use this editable worksheet in any notes app. No account or purchase is required. This is a boundary-setting exercise, not a weather forecast or a permit determination.

## Workflow
1. Read `fictional-brief.md`. Name the action the answer must support, its audience and deadline.
2. Fill `research-record.txt` without asking a tool to invent missing facts. Compare with `worked-example.md` only after your first attempt.
3. Check each source card in `source-cards.md` against place, period, meaning and affected group. These cards are self-authored fictional practice documents; they are not real opened evidence.
4. If an AI-assisted search tool is available, paste the bounded question and the instruction in the record. Label its output “Generated — not yet verified”. Do not ask it to find real facts about fictional Riverside Commons.
5. For a real location of your own, open relevant source pages yourself, record exact page title/URL/access date and what the page actually supports. Do not record the tool summary as opened evidence. Leave unanswered questions unresolved.
6. Without AI search, use an ordinary search engine with the same saved boundary visible in your notes. Open relevant pages manually.
7. Use `self-check.md`; if the boundary is flawed, record the revision and reason rather than suppress contradictory information.

You need not run a live search to complete the fictional exercise. Its assessable outcome is the bounded question, honest boundary checks and a stopping condition. A later lesson builds the search plan.

## Files
- `fictional-brief.md`: authoritative exercise facts and explicit unknowns.
- `research-record.txt`: blank learner template, including tool instruction.
- `worked-example.md`: one defensible answer, not the only wording.
- `source-cards.md`: controlled relevance practice and expected checks.
- `self-check.md`: completion checklist and revision record.

All examples are self-authored, fictional and permission-cleared for Academy practice. Do not enter private group member details, credentials or financial information into external tools. Interfaces vary; use the available search box and your preferred notes app. No application screenshot or real-world result is claimed by these text files.
