# Your worksheet
Use only fictional source notes. Keep answers local and unsent.

## Before drafting
- What happened, without a judgment about motive?
- What is the practical impact?
- What TWO outcomes may be requested?
- Which details are missing? Which statement is an interpretation?
- Which identifiers can be replaced with placeholders?

## Three alternatives
Write a concise/direct version, a warm/firm version, and a formal/neutral version. They must preserve the same facts and request. Label each version. Do not invent names, deadlines or evidence. If a warm opening assumes circumstances not established in the notes, mark that assumption separately or remove it.

## Sentence-by-sentence review
For every sentence, record the source fact or mark it as a request, placeholder or optional unverified assumption. Remove unsupported accusations, motives, timing, consequences, urgency and promises. Restore any missing alternative.

## Flawed draft exercise
For A and B in `05-draft-review-exercise.txt`, identify each unsupported change and propose the smallest correction. Then identify whether your chosen version contains any remaining assumptions.

## Final control
Write the approved wording below. Keep recipient empty. Record: facts checked; no deadline supplied; no attachment supplied; not sent. Review again after copying into any draft window.
