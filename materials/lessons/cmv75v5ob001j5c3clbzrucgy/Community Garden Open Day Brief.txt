Community Garden Open Day Brief
Fictional permission-cleared Academy practice example

The Community Garden Open Day begins at 10:00. Visitors can join a guided walk through the growing beds and a short composting demonstration. The event is intended for local residents who want to learn about the garden.

Volunteers should bring a reusable water bottle and wear suitable closed shoes. Children must remain with their accompanying adult. Activities may change if weather conditions make the growing beds unsafe.

The event date, street address, booking process, finish time and accessibility arrangements are not supplied in this practice brief. Do not invent them.
