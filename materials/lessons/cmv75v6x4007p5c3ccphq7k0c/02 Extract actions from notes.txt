Extract actions from notes — confirmed actions separate from suggestions

WHEN TO USE
Turn permission-cleared notes into an inspectable action list.

REQUIRED INPUTS
[NOTES], [DEFINITION OF ACTION], [OUTPUT COLUMNS]

REUSABLE INSTRUCTION
Use only the supplied notes. Apply the supplied definition of an action and use the requested output columns. Separate suggestions from confirmed actions. Do not invent owners or deadlines; write Not supplied when missing. If notes conflict, expose the conflict instead of choosing.

FICTIONAL SAMPLE INPUT
NOTES: Amara agreed to print the garden map by Friday. The team agreed to check spare gloves; no owner or deadline was recorded. Someone suggested buying a new sign, but no decision was made. DEFINITION: explicitly agreed task. COLUMNS: Task | Owner | Deadline | Source phrase.

GENERATED SAMPLE OUTPUT — AI-generated teaching draft, not evidence of an external action or measured assistant trial
Confirmed actions
Print garden map | Amara | Friday | Amara agreed to print the garden map by Friday.
Check spare gloves | Not supplied | Not supplied | The team agreed to check spare gloves.
Suggestions, not confirmed actions
Buy a new sign | suggestion only; no decision.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKS
Every task and owner is supported by the source; missing fields remain Not supplied; sign suggestion is not promoted to a commitment.

TEST RECORD
See test-cases.json for normal/missing/awkward cases. Record actual outputs and observations in test-log.csv; these are not pre-run model results. Keep failed outputs and exact smallest instruction changes.

KNOWN LIMITATION
Cannot infer decisions from ambiguous discussion.

MANUAL FALLBACK
