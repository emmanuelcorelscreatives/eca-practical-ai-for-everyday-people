# My decision check
1. Which important facts did I inspect directly, and where?
2. Which statements are seller claims rather than independent verification?
3. What do I value now? Which preference changed in the follow-up?
4. Which constraints are satisfied, not satisfied or unknown for each option?
5. Which unresolved question could change my conclusion?
6. Did any AI response invent evidence, treat a preference as mandatory, or hide an unknown?
7. Can I reproduce the reasoning without an assistant?

Record evidence / interpretation / my decision separately. You may defer your decision while information is missing.
