# Fictional community workshop communication guide
Self-authored practice preferences, not a real organization's policy.

Usual greeting: a simple name or “Hi Jordan” is acceptable for an established working contact. A first or official contact can use “Dear Jordan.” Ask or follow a stated preference if known.
Level of formality: clear, courteous, concise language; extra elaborate wording is not automatically respectful. Friendly wording must not imply friendship.
Urgent requests: place the action and actual deadline prominently. Do not invent emergencies, blame or consequences. “Please confirm” can be direct and polite.
The original source notes do not establish a relationship. The worksheet explicitly supplies an established-working-contact hypothetical for its choice exercise. Alternatives remain valid if meaning and the stated brief are preserved.
