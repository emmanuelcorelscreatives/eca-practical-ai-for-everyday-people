# Choose tone without changing meaning
Goal: compare friendly, formal and direct versions; justify your choice from relationship, purpose, expectations and urgency. These are comparison labels, not universal rules. Do not choose from age, appearance, nationality, language or stereotypes.

1. Read source-notes.json. Write a fixed-fact list: recipient, room, workshop date, confirmation deadline, reason. Dates are intentionally different. Unknown personal history stays unknown.
2. Write a four-line brief. For the lesson's selection exercise, Jordan is an established working contact; purpose is confirmation; expectation is clear and courteous; deadline matters but it is not an emergency. This is an explicit hypothetical scenario, not a fact inferred from the original notes. Consult communication-guide.md.
3. Draft three concise versions by hand. Change greeting, sentence length, contractions, formality and closing, while keeping the facts and requested action. If you choose a real writing assistant, paste only these fictional notes and this instruction: “Create three clearly labelled drafts: Friendly, Formal, and Direct. The reader is a community venue coordinator. The purpose is to request confirmation of the Cedar Room. Preserve every name, date, request, reason, and commitment. Do not invent personal details, emotions, compliments, history, or promises. Keep each version concise.” Record actual responses separately and treat them as unchecked drafts. No account is needed for the manual route.
4. Compare your versions with authored-drafts.json. Identify one warmth cue, one formality cue and one action cue. Neither polished writing nor selecting a draft is approval. Choose a version for the hypothetical brief and explain why another version could also work.
5. Check all five facts word by word. Highlight Jordan, Cedar Room, 22 October 2026, 16 October 2026, and the reason. Then inspect additions: no invented relationship, promise, emotion, history or consequence. Compare “could you please confirm whether” with “please confirm”; courtesy must not hide the required action or deadline.
6. Open flawed-draft.txt. Mark every unsupported detail and timing change; note which fixed details disappeared. Write a targeted correction instruction, then repair the draft yourself. “Make it better” does not identify errors.
7. Paste your selected draft into an ordinary document. Read the entire text, including closing, aloud if useful. Check action, visible deadline, accurate reason, appropriate relationship, and no new commitment. Preview a narrow phone-width window. If shortening, retain both dates, room, request, recipient and reason. Do not send anything as part of this exercise.
8. Without a writing assistant, duplicate your original draft, manually adjust greeting/sentence length/contractions/formality/closing, then compare to the fixed-fact list. This is the same meaning check with no generation step.

Completion checks: three meaning-preserving versions; a four-factor choice explanation; a five-fact audit plus unsupported-additions audit; a repaired flawed example; a complete destination-format reading. Use answers.md after completing your attempts. Keep revision of your personal style for the next lesson, Preserve Your Voice.
