# Fresh final-export review
1. Compare every important word, number and punctuation mark with approved-brief.md.
2. Inspect object support, joins, straight edges, repeated patterns, perspective, light and crop boundaries.
3. Compare original and edited versions; identify every change, including unintended nearby ones.
4. Check identity and representation where people appear; do not invent demographic facts.
5. Remove unsupported claims and preserve the synthetic-origin qualifier.
6. Provide a useful equivalent text alternative. For the checked fixture: “Synthetic practice poster for Riverside Reading Day, Saturday 24 October, 10:00–14:00 at Community Hall; free entry. Illustrated books rest on a table. Accessibility has not been confirmed.”
7. Export a new file; reopen the export rather than trust the editor preview. Check at phone/feed size for legibility, clipping and contrast. Test actual export settings in your own editor.
8. Keep original, edited source, export and completed decision log separately. Mark ACCEPT, CORRECT or REJECT with evidence. Uncertainty means investigate or reject, not an automatic pass.
