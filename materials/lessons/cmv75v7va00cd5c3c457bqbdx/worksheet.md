# Preserve Your Voice — practical worksheet
Allow about 20 minutes of optional practice. Use an ordinary editor or a general assistant. Nothing is sent externally by this pack.

1. Read owned-sample.txt. Confirm that the pack is self-authored fictional training text. For a personal replacement, choose a complete paragraph with a similar audience and purpose; remove confidential details and record permission. If unsure, use this supplied sample.
2. Make a table with columns trait, exact phrase, and keep/change/delete. Find one direct opening, one optional request and one closing. Decide whether “warm,” “always humorous,” and “contractions” are observations supported by this particular sample. Separate evidence from your preferred future style.
3. Read approved-preferences.md and edit it to your taste. Keep five practical preferences. Change one for a formal audience without claiming either checklist defines your personality.
4. Open source-notes.txt. Mark date, time, place, limit, optionality, tea, accessibility purpose and both missing fields. Keep these facts distinct from the garden-room sample. The event is fictional and dated; do not announce it as current.
5. Draft one announcement under 100 words using your approved preferences. Optional AI route: use prompts.md and retain the actual response. Manual route: write a direct opening, one optional request, tea and accessibility lines, missing-field note and plain closing. Keep both unknowns visible in your working record.
6. Use meaning-check.csv to compare each statement against the notes. Check date, time and quantity character by character. Check whether an invented feeling, promise, superlative, name or contact has appeared. Record evidence beside every pass; leave unresolved gaps pending.
7. Read flawed-draft.txt. Find at least seven meaning errors and one voice issue. Repair it using only source notes. Do not “fix” missing contact details by guessing.
8. Try changing “If you have an accessibility question” to “For accessibility questions.” Keep the organizer as the contact route and the missing method visible. Read your version aloud; revise any word that feels unnatural. Decide whether to keep “No pressure; come as you are.”
9. Save separate records: source notes, approved preferences, raw draft (if generated), final personally revised draft and a completed fact check. Check answers.md after your attempt. An answer can pass meaning while using a different closing.

On a phone, keep the five-item checklist above the draft in a notes app. Edit one sentence at a time manually. Before accepting any version ask: Is every supplied fact included? Has meaning changed? Does this sound natural? What would I rewrite myself?

Completion: all supplied facts preserved; no unsupported statements; missing organizer name and contact method retained in the working record; fewer than 100 words; personally approved preferences with evidence; one manual revision explained. The announcement remains a practice draft until a genuine organizer supplies necessary contact information.
