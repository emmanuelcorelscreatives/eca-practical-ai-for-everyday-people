# Choose the Right Depth of Review — Lesson 67

This offline practice kit is self-authored fictional teaching material. Nothing here is a real quote, booking, AI session or authorization. Use a text reader, calculator, and optionally a spreadsheet. No account or external action is needed. Currency-neutral “units” are fictional.

## Start with use and consequences

Light: brainstorm possibilities that nobody will rely on yet. Check usefulness, fit with the brief and obvious problems. Standard: review an ordinary draft for use or sharing; compare important supplied details, meaning, completeness and permission to share. Strong: before a consequential fact or action, open suitable evidence, check exact support and scope, rerun arithmetic, reveal gaps and preserve human approval. A short polished answer can need strong review. A long idea list can need light review.

## Exercise 1 — One draft, two purposes

Read input.md, sources.md and teaching-output.md. First use only theme ideas in a private planning discussion. Name the depth, check each theme against the brief, discard one poor fit and record what your acceptance covers. Then consider the booking recommendation. State who an error affects, how reversible it is and which depth is required. A previous light acceptance cannot approve action.

## Exercise 2 — Inspect and calculate

Open the venue note and catering quote in sources.md rather than relying on the teaching draft. Identify date, room and layout scope. Compare 40 attendees with 36 seated places. Independently calculate 40 × 12, then 10% of that subtotal, then the total. In a spreadsheet enter labels in A2:A6 and values/formulas in B2:B6: Quantity/40, Unit price/12, Subtotal/=B2*B3, Service charge/10%, Total/=B4*(1+B5). Calculator fallback: 40 × 12 = 480; 480 × 0.10 = 48; 480 + 48 = 528. Check both formulas and source values; a correct result does not prove availability.

Optional reproducible code: run `python3 calculate.py` in a copied kit folder. It prints the arithmetic and seating gap only, without making a booking or checking real-world facts. See expected-checks.md for exact expected lines.

## Exercise 3 — Decide and preserve a record

Complete decision-record.md with intended use, affected people, consequence, chosen depth, opened evidence, checks, correction, unresolved gaps and decision. Keep booking paused. Draft questions for the venue about availability, accessibility, cancellation terms and capacity alternatives; ask an authorized person about permission to commit funds. Do not contact anyone during this exercise. Save input, teaching draft, sources, calculation and final decision together.

## Exercise 4 — Standard review and transfer

Read standard-draft.md, compare it with its supplied brief and correct the draft before sharing. Then complete the three rows in depth-practice.csv. Explain why each depth fits the intended use. For three recent low-risk outputs of your own, repeat Use → Consequence → Depth → Check → Decision without exposing private content. Compare your work with answers.md after attempting it.

Success means distinguishing ideas from evidence, finding the seat shortfall and omitted charge, retaining unknowns, and stating precisely what may be used. This lesson introduces a short decision record; the next lesson builds a full claim-and-evidence table.
