# Required-input check
- Has the stated trigger actually happened?
- Are event name, confirmed date AND time, location or meeting link, intended audience, approved description, response method and review owner present and usable?
- Are any fields contradictory? Ask the owner rather than resolve uncertainty by guessing.
- Can important generated details be compared against the supplied information?
- Is a person identified to review the result?

A suggested image is optional when a text-only draft is allowed. A vague date such as “sometime next month” is not confirmed. Present does not necessarily mean trustworthy or usable.
