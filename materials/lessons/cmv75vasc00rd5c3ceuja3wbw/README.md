# Lesson 166 — Use Synthetic Narration Responsibly
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 28

This is a self-authored, fictional practice exercise. Riverside Community Garden is an invented teaching example; no real organization or speaker has approved a recording. No voice sample, private information or celebrity identity is supplied.

## Outcome
Prepare a short announcement with an authorized general synthetic voice, check every spoken word, disclose its synthetic origin, and name a manual fallback. Keep publishing under deliberate human control.

## Files and order
1. Read `announcement.txt` and `exercise.md`.
2. Before uploading anything, complete `rights-register.csv` using the actual current service terms and intended audience/use; record unknowns honestly. Permission for text is not permission for a person's voice, music or images.
3. Use the service's authorized provider-supplied general narrator. Do not select or create a clone of an identifiable person. Preview clarity and tone, not resemblance. Leave style controls unchanged initially.
4. Generate and listen while reading the script. Record actual observations in `listening-review.csv`; do not prefill a pass. Check names, times, words, omissions, pauses and emphasis. Regenerate only the affected passage if supported and compare again.
5. Compare headphones and an ordinary phone speaker. Ask a second person the unprompted questions in `listener-review.md`.
6. Use the plain disclosure in `disclosure.txt` near the player or description; ensure corrected captions match the final accepted audio.
7. Retain the final script, accepted audio, provider/licence information, permission records and observations together. Complete `acceptance-checklist.md`. Nothing here sends or publishes audio.

## Important boundary
A waveform shows audio exists; it proves neither accuracy nor permission. Metadata may be lost and cannot substitute for permission, disclosure or human review. Check trusted local guidance appropriate to your actual intended use. If authorization is uncertain, the service is unavailable or delivery keeps failing, record yourself reading this script or share text/captioned silent media.

## Reproducibility
`python3 check_kit.py` verifies the exact lesson announcement and complete blank worksheet schemas. This checks the kit, not listening accuracy, licensing or authorization. No package downloads, accounts or network are needed to run it.
