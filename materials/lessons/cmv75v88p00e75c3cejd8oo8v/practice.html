1. Notebook question

A notebook costs 24 tokens. What is the total cost of 3 identical notebooks?

Hint 1 — direction

How could you split 24 into two easier parts before multiplying each part by 3?

Hint 2 — one step

Split 24 into 20 and 4. Multiply each part by 3.

Hint 3 — stronger clue

20 × 3 = 60. Work out 4 × 3, then combine the products.

Answer — open after attempting

24 = 20 + 4; 20 × 3 = 60; 4 × 3 = 12; 60 + 12 = 72 tokens.

Check whether your split preserves 24 and both partial products are correct. Use a calculator independently.

2. Fresh question — no hints

A packet contains 36 cards. How many cards are in 4 identical packets?

Check only after finishing

36 = 30 + 6; 30 × 4 = 120; 6 × 4 = 24; 120 + 24 = 144 cards.

Verify 36 × 4 independently with a calculator.

3. Main idea from a source

Fictional source: Residents turned an unused courtyard into a shared garden. Volunteers cleared rubbish, planted vegetables, and created a weekly watering schedule. The space now supplies produce for community meals.

Feedback guide — after your sentence

Does your sentence include the shared action, transformation and purpose? Compare every claim with the paragraph. Revise yourself before opening the example.

One possible revision — after revising

Residents worked together to transform an unused courtyard into a garden that supports community meals.

Other faithful wording is valid. No budget, motive or named individual is supplied.
