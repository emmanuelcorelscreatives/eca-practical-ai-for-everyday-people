# Learner submission checklist
Choose a low-stakes everyday topic and a source you have permission to use. Define a real intended learner, language variety, tone and purpose. Do not add private personal information.

Submit:
- Original source and permission/origin note, separately from drafts.
- Instructions/tool support/account-access check or manual route.
- Explanation checked directly against protected source details.
- Four practice situations and source-derived answer key.
- Bilingual glossary with usage notes and unresolved terms.
- Accessible summary preserving all warnings and conditions.
- Review record with actual reviewer/date/evidence, one correction propagated across all parts, assumptions and limitations.
- Listening route record, including actual observations of dates/times/pauses or a clear “not tested” status.

Do not mark human review or speech testing complete without doing it. Consequential or public translations need suitably capable human review. Use specific pronunciation feedback as rehearsal guidance, not a proficiency score. Identify one improvement for next time.
