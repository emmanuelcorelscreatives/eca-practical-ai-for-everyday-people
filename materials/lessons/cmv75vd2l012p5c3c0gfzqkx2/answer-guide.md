# Expected planning checks
Problem: difficulty locating agreed actions and preparing follow-up after permitted community meetings. Beneficiary: volunteer coordinator. Exclusions: recording, sending, inventing owners and deciding policy.
The manual baseline lists reading, highlighting, copying, drafting and reviewing, with actual time/corrections/questions blank. Inputs include permission-cleared notes, date, approved labels, known deadlines, references and destination. Exclude credentials, private contact details, unrelated discussion and unnecessary identifiers.
A usable brief defines jobs before brands and leaves account/cost fields blank. The output is a reviewed package with an action table, unresolved questions, follow-up draft and visible status. A blank template is not a generated result.
Acceptance decision examples: unsupported owner or deadline = correction; unavailable notes or permission = stop/request; missing owner/deadline in otherwise complete notes = mark MISSING and flag question; suggestion presented as decision = correction; unreadable output = recreate from checked content; no coordinator approval = hold sharing. Trace each action to a source location; preserve meaning; inspect saved artifact in destination.
The manual fallback must cover reading, highlighting confirmed decisions, copying actions, marking missing fields, drafting follow-up, comparing with notes and putting it in the review folder. Reviewer checks facts, meaning, permissions, recipients, attachments and readiness, and can stop. No sending occurs as part of preparing the brief.
Normal and missing-information cases remain plans. No speed improvement, test pass, live price, real AI output or final approval is claimed. An alternative personal problem is acceptable when recurring, bounded, supplied with suitable inputs and observable success. Narrow vague or uncheckable ideas before choosing tools.
