# Standard-review exercise
All content below is fictional and self-authored.

Supplied brief: A private planning group will meet on 18 November 2026 at 14:00 in Library Meeting Room B. Bring one theme suggestion. This is a planning meeting, not the workshop itself. The notice is for that group only; no booking or workshop date has been confirmed.

Unchecked draft: “The workshop is confirmed for 19 November at 14:00 in Room A. Bring specialist equipment. Share this invitation publicly.”

Task: Choose a review depth for a corrected ordinary internal notice. Compare event meaning, date, room, request, audience and permission with the brief. Correct every mismatch. If the message later makes a consequential commitment, reassess depth before acting.
