# Organize by Task and Outcome

Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 8, Lesson 43

Name a saved instruction for the useful job it performs and the outcome it produces. This exercise changes titles only; keep each instruction body unchanged. These are self-authored fictional practice notes, not private correspondence or records.

## Practice

1. Open `practice-library-before.json` in a text editor, or copy its three title/body pairs into a new notes app or document. Google Keep is the demonstrated application in the lesson; the exercise also works in a plain document. Use only new Academy practice notes, not existing personal notes.
2. Read the body of “AI stuff.” Identify its action, input and outcome. Rename it **Turn rough notes into a short checklist**. Do not edit the body.
3. Close or hide the body. From the title alone, can you tell when to use the instruction and what result to expect?
4. Repeat for “Writing” and “Research.” Compare with `answer-key.md` only after trying your own names. Equivalent accurate titles are welcome; the suggested wording is not a rigid formula.
5. Search your practice library for **checklist**. Confirm the helpful word is part of the task title.
6. Optionally test the first instruction with `community-garden-notes.txt` in an available assistant. Paste the exact saved body followed by the fictional input. Label any result **Generated material — check against the notes**. An assistant response is not evidence of a real event.
7. Use `output-check.md` to compare every supplied detail. The date is missing; do not invent it. The manually authored checklist in `answer-key.md` illustrates the checks; it is not a captured model response.
8. Rename three of your own permission-cleared prompts. Keep the bodies unchanged today. A clear title never makes sensitive material safe to upload.

## Manual fallback

Open `plain-document-library.md`, which shows the same reusable instructions under task headings. Or use the two-column `task-library.csv`. No special notes-app organization feature is required. A tool-dependent instruction should keep its job as the title and record its feature requirement inside the body; that does not imply every assistant supports the feature.

## Completion checks

- Three useful job/outcome titles; no product-only or vague category titles.
- Original instruction bodies preserved exactly.
- Hidden-body title test answers both “when?” and “what result?”
- All garden input details traceable; no invented date, attendee count or completed external action.
