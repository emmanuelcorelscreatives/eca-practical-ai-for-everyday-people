- Content: every required page/slide/sheet and fact present? Meaning preserved? Missing information visible?
- Numbers: independently recomputed important totals, formulas, units and references? Evidence: __________
- Layout: every edge, line, image and page break checked at ordinary and enlarged size? Reading order usable?

- Destination: actual saved file opened? Links tested where supplied? Actual phone/projection/print context tested or clearly recorded as untested?

Correction log: expected / found / evidence / editable change / new export / observed recheck.
Unresolved fields and unavailable tests: __________
Filename of opened re-export: __________  Repaired and neighboring content checked: __________

Never check a box based solely on the chat response, editable draft or success notification. A placeholder is not evidence of a functioning link. Keep factual unknowns visible.
