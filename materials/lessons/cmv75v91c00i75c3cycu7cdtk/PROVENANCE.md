# Provenance and preservation
All workshop conversation and the optional generated draft are self-authored fictional Academy teaching material. The WAV files are actual eSpeak-generated synthetic practice recordings matching the authored workshop exchanges. They are not real meeting evidence, actual person voices, the course narrator, a real transcription observation or actual assistant output. Exact generation provenance and PCM sample offsets are in fixture-provenance.json; fixture-transcript.txt is the exact synthetic source script. The seeded book/look error is a teaching fixture corrected only after actual playback, never a claimed observed ASR error. No external data collection, messages or bookings occur. Original lecture, instructor and production settings are preserved.
