# Evidence practice worksheet

For each candidate record exact option, required date, quantity/occupancy, availability and full payable cost. Separate mandatory costs from optional extras. Keep the source and fixed example time visible; record assumptions and unresolved fields explicitly.

- Why does a family-page price of 43 fail the requested product?
- Why is one available item insufficient even at 51 each?
- What remains unknown before entering a delivery area?
- Calculate the backpack total without and with gift wrap.
- Normalize Harbour House's 90 nightly rate to the two-night stay before adding mandatory costs.
- What is the hotel total without and with breakfast?
- Which cancellation detail remains unresolved?
- Why does an open-shop directory card fail to prove exact stock?
- Refresh the local product state. What must you recheck before using the old comparison row?

Do not enter a real address, traveller identity, card or account. No booking or purchase is needed or possible here.
