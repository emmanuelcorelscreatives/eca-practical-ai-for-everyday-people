# Seven checks before approval
- Names and speaker labels match source context.
- Dates and numbers are supported; 24 October 2026 is Saturday.
- Decisions remain distinct from suggestions.
- Tasks, owners and deadlines are supported; missing fields are unconfirmed.
- Conditions, qualifications and disagreements remain visible.
- Unclear details stay labeled; replay surrounding sentences and ask for confirmation.
- Recipients, purpose, exact version and attachments are appropriate.

Approve only supported content for a confirmed audience. Revise or request confirmation for missing facts. Stop if content, permission or destination cannot be established. Recording authorization does not automatically authorize distribution. A correct summary is not permission to share everything.
