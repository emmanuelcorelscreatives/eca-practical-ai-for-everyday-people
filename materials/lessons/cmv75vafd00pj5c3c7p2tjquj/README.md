# Lesson 155 — Edit for Readability and Accessibility
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

This self-authored fictional community clean-up briefing pack supports checking the artifact readers receive. It is a classroom practice pack, not an announcement of a real event. No dates, venues, contact details, attendance statistics or approvals are invented. Bracketed missing values stay visible until a responsible person confirms them.

Read approved-source-pack.md, then exercise.md. Use briefing-outline.md and five-slide-outline.md in your actual document/presentation editor. Export your own files and fill export-review.csv from observations of those exports, not from the editing canvas. concise-wording.md supplies a meaning-preserving edit exercise. answer-key.md explains expected checks, not a claim that your file has passed.

An attractive page or successful export does not establish accessibility. Check contrast, enlargement, meaningful headings and order, text equivalents and concise wording; repeat after every new export. A fixed-layout PDF may not reflow correctly in every viewer. If it clips, overlaps, requires difficult navigation or exposes poor structure, record the failure and repair the export or use a simpler accessible format. Do not claim universal PDF reflow.

This pack supplies planning and review material; it does not pretend a PDF/tag tree, keyboard test, screen-reader result, contrast measurement or peer review has already been executed. No single font size or automated checker guarantees success for every reader.
