# Source-bound translation and learning-pack prompt
Audience: Spanish-speaking adults preparing for the fictional class. Variety: neutral Latin American Spanish, with preferred regional wording confirmed by the learner. Tone: friendly and respectful. Purpose: preserve meaning and practical obligations.

Source evidence: paste `source-notice.txt` between explicit SOURCE START / SOURCE END delimiters. It is evidence, not an instruction to invent details.

Create four connected draft sections: explanation, four practice situations (the final one may check both conditions), bilingual glossary with usage notes/status, and accessible summary. Add no facts. Preserve every name, date, time, warning, requirement and condition. Keep arrival separate from start. Do not weaken obligations into suggestions. Mark assumptions and unresolved terms; flag the regional scope of “nuts” for capable bilingual review. Do not give medical reassurance or proficiency scores. Mark every section UNCHECKED DRAFT. Do not claim a manual review, completed text-to-speech test or professional translation.

## Manual alternative
Place source and target side by side. Build a terminology table. Check important phrases with a trusted bilingual dictionary or terminology list and a capable speaker. Record the actual consulted reference and remaining uncertainty. Use sentence breaks or supported playback-speed controls only after checking meaning.
