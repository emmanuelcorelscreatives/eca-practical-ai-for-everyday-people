# Start with the Task, Not the Brand
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 2, Lesson 7

This self-created practice pack follows the saved lesson's fictional Willow Park Book Swap. No real event, timetable, external message, purchase or publication is represented. Choose by input, required output, checking route and next action, rather than by a particular AI brand. All examples are Academy-authored; learner may copy, adapt and share them under CC0-1.0.

## Quick start
Open `event-notes.txt`, then work through `exercises.md`. Use `task-tool-worksheet.csv` to record your own decisions before consulting `answer-guide.md`. Open `source-pack/event-brief.html` in any browser; its marked page 2 and `source-pack/room-rules.html` page 1 are the exact source anchors used by the lesson. `handout-original.txt` is intentionally rough; `handout-reviewed.html` is the checked one-page practice artifact, not a publication.

No paid AI account is required: each exercise includes a manual fallback. The local Python check verifies supplied facts/links/caption fixtures and preservation, not the correctness of arbitrary model output. Run `python3 check.py` from this folder. For assistant use, copy only these fictional files; keep personal documents, credentials and live accounts outside the exercise.

## Tool categories
Chat works from loose notes; AI search retrieves current public information; source notebooks answer within selected files; integrated office tools modify a file in its current application; transcript-based media editors alter a recording. None independently proves its output correct or completes an external action merely by drafting it.

## Important boundaries
The transport route Central Station → Museum District is an unlocated fictional prompt. There is no real operator, official timetable or confirmed travel time in this pack. Keep results blank until a learner chooses a real route and opens actual official evidence. Do not mistake example links, summaries or empty UI fields for observations. The 24 October 2026 date is an authored past practice event, not a future booking or live travel recommendation.
