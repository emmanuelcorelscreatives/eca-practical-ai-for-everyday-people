# Lesson 163 — Plan a Short Audio or Video Message
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Stage 28

This kit supports one clear purpose, one requested response, supported facts and a natural timed read-through. All event notes are fictional Academy practice data. No real event, registration, recording, voice permission or publication is claimed.

Open `practice-editor.html` in a browser. It works offline: the supplied example is a deterministic **practice draft**, not a live AI service. Keep source notes separate from generated suggestions. Review the unsupported phrase before removing it. The date, time, place and contact must remain placeholders. Do not publish or send this invitation.

1. Read `source-notes.json`. Write purpose, audience, action and time target in `planning-worksheet.md`.
2. Copy `draft-instruction.txt` into the offline editor. Inspect the supplied draft. Identify the one unsupported request.
3. Compare `generated-practice-draft.txt` and `revised-practice-script.txt`. Preserve every supported fact and placeholder. Remove only the unsupported question request.
4. Read the revised script aloud at your natural pace and record your **actual** elapsed time in `read-through-log.csv`. Include pauses. Do not claim a timing from word count.
5. Ask another person to describe purpose and next action after one listen. Mark unclear wording. Check rights and missing details before recording or sharing.
6. Run `python3 verify_materials.py` to check this kit's facts, placeholders, single action and exercise fixtures. This checks data integrity, not your voice, actual timing or final publication readiness.

The lesson's just-under-forty-seconds example is an illustrative course example, not a measurement of this kit or a target students must reproduce. Your measured time may differ. If long, remove a separate idea before compressing words; do not rush. Without AI, any notes editor and an ordinary timer are sufficient.
