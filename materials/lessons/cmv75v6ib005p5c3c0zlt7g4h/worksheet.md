# Lesson 36 — Protect Meaning During Summarization

Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People

Practice goal: compare a summary with its source for missing conditions, exceptions, disagreements and unknowns. All Riverside garden material is fictional, permission-cleared Academy exercise content. It is not operational safety advice for a real event.

1. Open source-plan.md (or Riverside Community Garden Weekend Plan.docx). Keep it unchanged. Read all six sections and establish the source as evidence.
2. Open flawed-summary.md. It is an intentionally flawed teaching specimen, not observed AI output. Before looking at the answer key, complete audit-template.csv: mark whether each source point survives, whether wording is accurate, and what correction is needed.
3. Optional: use prompts.md in a file-capable assistant. Verify title, headings and unavailable sections after upload. Compare against the actual file. If unsupported, paste only this fictional source or use the manual workflow; there is no required subscription or online action.
4. Compare the whole surrounding source passage for each row. Look for if, unless, except, only, may, however and disagreed, but do not rely on keywords alone: recommendations can conflict without a keyword.
5. Revise only the errors in your summary. Preserve both inspection/access conditions, the exact weather substitution, trained-coordinator exception, attributed disagreement with no settled decision, and unconfirmed glove quantity. Do not make up quantities or confirmations.
6. Check your actual revision again against all six rows, then compare audit-answer-key.csv and checked-summary.md. Different concise wording is acceptable if every action-changing qualification survives. The reference summary including uncertainty labels is below 120 whitespace-separated words; word count alone does not test meaning.
7. Optional supported desktop Word workflow: Review > Compare > Compare. Select Riverside Community Garden Weekend Plan.docx as Original and Garden Volunteer Summary.docx as Revised. Review differences in the separate third comparison document; leave originals unchanged. Interfaces, account plans and browser/mobile availability vary. A revision mark shows a wording difference, not whether it changes meaning.
8. Manual fallback in any editor: place source and summary side by side and read each complete section against the matching audit row. Use Find for signal words, then inspect contextual qualifications not caught by Find.
9. Optional source notebook: open each cited passage and enough surrounding text to check attribution, scope, conditions, exceptions, uncertainty and contradiction. A citation or a second AI agreeing is not independent verification. The original source remains evidence.

## Completion checks
You should find four faulty source points in the intentionally flawed summary: weather detail, tool exception, disagreement/unrecorded decision, glove unknown. General plan and inspection/access are correct. Keep all six rows for the second pass. A checked summary must not imply the inspection or access confirmation already occurred.

Save the completed audit and checked draft separately from the source. For real consequential decisions involving safety, rights, money, health or commitments, use appropriate stronger review and expertise. Never convert this fictional example into instructions for a real garden event.
