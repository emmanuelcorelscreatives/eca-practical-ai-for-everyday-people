## Tuesday mini-example

“Can” became “will,” turning ability/possibility into a promise. “If the figures arrive by Monday” disappeared. Tuesday and reviewer survive, but those alone do not make the edit faithful. A faithful alternative: “If the figures arrive by Monday, I can review the draft on Tuesday.” Keep the condition or flag uncertainty; do not silently decide to promise an unconditional review.

## Acceptance decision

Pass your own edit only after you can point to source and draft evidence for every protected detail, resolve uncertainty, and read the result aloud comfortably. Otherwise restore the exact detail and check again. The shortest version is not automatically best. Keep the checked draft for the next lesson’s audience-tone work.
