# Create Form-Filling Checklists
Emmanuel Corels Academy · Practical AI for Everyday People · Lesson 129

All data and the grant application are self-created fictional practice materials. No real application, legal eligibility criteria or submission service is represented. No account, paid service or real personal data is required.

1. Open `Community_Learning_Grant_Application_SAMPLE.html` in a browser. Inspect all three pages. Print/save to PDF if your document assistant needs a PDF, preserving all three pages. This is the original for this exercise; keep it unchanged.
2. Read `applicant-record-SAMPLE.txt`. No proof of address is supplied. Having an email address does not establish a contact preference. Prior applications and access needs are unknown.
3. Open `checklist-starter.csv` in your preferred spreadsheet. Use the exact labels and source locations. Apply AVAILABLE, MISSING, UNCLEAR or NOT APPLICABLE only when supported. A blank never proves not applicable.
4. Optionally use `prompt.txt` with a permitted document assistant. Treat its extraction as generated material and compare each row against the original. Manual work is equally valid.
5. Compare `ocr-error-exercise.txt` with page 3. Correct ability bill to utility bill and record `OCR corrected after page-image check` in a separate verification note. Check spelling/date order against the applicant record; do not duplicate the name/date unnecessarily in the checklist.
6. Compare with `checklist-expected.csv` after doing the exercise. The added Requirement or condition column preserves required markers and contact choices; Verification notes records only checks actually performed. Seven data/document rows: AVAILABLE 3; MISSING 2; UNCLEAR 2; NOT APPLICABLE 0. The declaration is a separate applicant-only action, not an eighth completed data row. Compare the separate `applicant-actions-expected.csv`; its declaration stays UNRESOLVED.
7. Filter MISSING and UNCLEAR, and separately record the declaration as an unresolved applicant action. Ask about proof of address, contact preference, previous application and accessibility needs. Do not sign, answer declarations or submit.
8. Final review: every page inspected; exact labels/conditions preserved; evidence links retained; missing/unclear items visible; available values checked; no external action taken. Keep originals and checklist together with appropriate access controls, and remove unnecessary practice copies when done.

## Manual fallback
Read one section at a time. Copy exact requests and page references onto paper or a local document; mark available, missing or questionable. The same no-guessing and no-submission boundary applies.

## Files and scope
CSV files use UTF-8 and contain no macros or active links. `.invalid` email is intentionally non-deliverable. The HTML is a static printable three-page sample, not a vendor interface. The deliberately wrong OCR text is labeled as an exercise fault, not claimed as a live OCR result. Source lesson narration is preserved in the project; these materials explain its practice scenario without altering the lecture.
