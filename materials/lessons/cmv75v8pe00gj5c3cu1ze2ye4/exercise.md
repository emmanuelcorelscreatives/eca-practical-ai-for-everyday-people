# Lesson 101 practice: write a short evidence-based brief

Emmanuel Corels Academy — fictional low-stakes practice. No login or AI subscription needed.

1. Serve this folder with `python3 -m http.server 8765` and open `http://localhost:8765/index.html`. You may instead open the HTML files directly.
2. Write the bounded question and exclusions in a copy of `brief-template.md`. Leave Checked date empty until you inspect sources.
3. Open `search-request.html` and write a request asking for concise synthesis, direct links, source dates, caveats, conflicts, missing information and separately labelled evidence and interpretation. Read the unverified summary and independently open all three linked sources. Record each exact passage, publisher, date, scope, status and caveat in your own claim table.
4. Leave the source and perform the supplied `lateral-check.html` activity: distinguish publisher self-description from the separate simulated directory and blog. Record that actual external publisher identity and corroboration remain unverified. Keep the visitor-rules finding narrow: closed reusable water bottles, study rooms, open cups excluded. Do not write “all drinks” or extend it to the entire building.
5. Exclude the undated blog from the exact-rule finding. Retain the old quiet-room guide as an unresolved conflict. Do not invent room-label equivalence or signage inspection.
6. Write the six-part brief; label practical interpretation separately. Record your actual source-inspection date.
7. Open every source link from your finished brief, verify the destination and passage, and run the optional kit checker.
8. Compare with `completed-brief.html` and `claim-table.csv`. Explain why “appears consistent with” is better bounded than “is always allowed”.

## Manual fallback
Use the supplied pages and notes without AI. For a new real-world low-stakes question, search ordinary web sources yourself and repeat the source-by-source checks. This fictional kit makes no live web-search or current-policy claims.

## Expected checks
- All six sections present; closed reusable bottles and study-room scope retained.
- Visitor rule has its direct passage link, source date and exception.
- Blog excluded, old-guide conflict visible, room/signage questions unanswered.
- Checked date records actual inspection, not source publication.
- Consequential real decisions require the original authority and appropriately qualified help.
