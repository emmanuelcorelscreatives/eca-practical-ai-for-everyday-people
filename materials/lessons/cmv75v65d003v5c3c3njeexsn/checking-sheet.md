# Draft checking sheet
Keep the original notes open beside the draft. Check actual output rather than assuming an assistant followed the request.

## Source facts
- [ ] Venue is exactly Riverside Community Hall.
- [ ] Date is Saturday, 14 November; no unsupported year was added.
- [ ] Time is exactly 10:00–13:00.
- [ ] Accepted items are clean books, toys, or kitchen items.
- [ ] “No electrical items” is preserved.
- [ ] No contact, registration link/process, fee, address or other unknown detail was invented.

## Brief and usable shape
- [ ] Correct job: a public announcement for local residents.
- [ ] Friendly, direct wording appropriate to a noticeboard and messaging group.
- [ ] One headline.
- [ ] One introductory sentence.
- [ ] Exactly four bullet points.
- [ ] One closing action.
- [ ] Maximum 100 words (count the actual draft in your editor, including headings and placeholder text).
- [ ] Necessary missing details marked [DETAIL NEEDED] rather than guessed.
- [ ] Before sharing: unresolved placeholder removed or replaced only with a verified fact.
