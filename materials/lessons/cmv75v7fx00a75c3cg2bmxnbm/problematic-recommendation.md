# Deliberately flawed practice draft — AUTHORED EXAMPLE

This is a self-authored illustration of the flawed reasoning described in the lesson. It is not an actual captured assistant response and is not source evidence. The claims below are intentional errors to identify, not advice to apply.

| Person | Draft assignment | Unsupported draft explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mina | Welcome desk | Younger volunteers are naturally energetic and good with technology. |
| Daniel | Move furniture | Men are usually better suited to heavy setup. |
| Aisha | Photography | Her accent may make greeting visitors difficult. |
| Ravi | Information desk | Parents are naturally patient and organized. |

Find both unsupported identity assumptions and contradictions of recorded facts. A polite or complimentary explanation can still allocate visibility, choice and burdens unfairly. Do not assume these labels are true of the fictional people just because this flawed draft asserts them.
