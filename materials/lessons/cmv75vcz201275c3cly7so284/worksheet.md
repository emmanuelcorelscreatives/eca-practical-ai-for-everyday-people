# One-workflow cleanup exercise
Allow about ten minutes for one pass; this is a suggested exercise length, not a fixed review schedule.

1. Open Sources/Workshop_Brief_Current.txt and Sources/Venue_Guide_Saved_Copy.txt. Compare purpose, owner, scope and last checked. Why does the older guide fail this task? Would age alone justify retiring a source? Move only a working copy to the archive; preserve history.
2. Open Sources/Generated_Summary_Assumptions.txt. Identify two claims unsupported by the opened brief. Record the missing evidence in Sources/Replacement_Source_Record.txt, leaving the evidence field empty until verified. Do not ask AI to invent a replacement.
3. Inspect Permissions/Account_Checklist.csv. Identify account type, purpose, granted scope and current need. Record the proposed action and verified status separately. For the managed connection, name the administrator as owner and retain an unresolved request. No actual account is connected here.
4. Compare Prompts/Workshop_Update_v1.txt with the current brief. Identify the instruction that could invent a commitment. Read Prompts/Workshop_Update_Current.txt and preserve the old copy in Archive_Not_For_Current_Use.
5. Optionally paste the current template and Examples/Test_Missing_Venue.txt into a tool you are permitted to use, or perform the same check manually. Record your actual output separately. Compare with Outputs/Expected_Checks.txt: missing venue must stop a shareable announcement. Do not label the supplied expected check as an observed model response.
6. Repeat with Examples/Test_Complete_Input.txt. Check the date, audience and supplied venue word for word. A draft still needs a human check of recipient and destination before any sharing. Do not send it for this exercise.
7. Complete Review_Log/Blank.csv with exactly seven fields. Use a review trigger in the next-review-date field when no date is appropriate, or mark a chosen date as provisional. Review sooner when information changes quickly or errors matter more. Avoid a universal schedule.
8. Validate retained source opens, current template stops on missing information, and unresolved access has a named owner. If any fail, pause and use the manual fallback below.

Manual fallback: open the approved brief yourself; copy only confirmed date and audience into a private draft; leave venue blank, mark REQUIRED INFORMATION, and ask the source owner. Do not reuse the retired guide or connection. For phone permissions, inspect the real categories and needed features; restore only necessary access if a required feature breaks, or use a manual route. No phone UI is simulated here.

Self-check: explain what evidence would permit changing revoke requested to revoked; why deleting shared data needs a separate check; and what event will trigger your next review. Compare your decisions with answer-key.md.
