# Review before approval and again before any real sending
- Does each factual claim match the notes?
- Is the booking change Tuesday to Thursday?
- Is notice described as Monday afternoon, without an invented hour or “late” claim?
- Are two volunteers unavailable Thursday?
- Does the request retain BOTH Tuesday restoration and another evening?
- Have motives, accusations, fabricated evidence, threats and consequences been excluded?
- Did the draft add urgency, a deadline or an unauthorized commitment?
- Are unknown recipient/sender details placeholders rather than inventions?
- Are assumptions shown separately, not smuggled into polished wording?
- Is any attachment genuinely present, correct and authorized? In this exercise none is supplied; do not claim one.
- Does tone fit the actual relationship? Formal is not automatically better.
- After copying, are all words and formatting intact?
- Is the recipient correct? In this fictional exercise leave it empty and DO NOT SEND.

Calm language does not establish truth, agreement or responsibility. For a serious real dispute follow established procedures and seek appropriate qualified support.
