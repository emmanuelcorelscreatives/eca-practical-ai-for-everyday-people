# Answer key and expected checks
## Evidence, not identity
“Quick update” gives the purpose immediately. “if you can” and “No pressure” soften the request. Four sentences and plain everyday words support concise, practical structure. “Warm” is a reasonable interpretation to approve or reject. “Always humorous” is unsupported. There are no contractions in the supplied sample; do not call them an observed feature. You may still choose “contractions when natural” as a future preference. Never infer a personality or copy a different real person’s identity from these clues.

Manual editing uses the same five-item checklist; no AI feature or subscription is necessary. Do not replace “up to five” with “five” while shortening a sentence.
