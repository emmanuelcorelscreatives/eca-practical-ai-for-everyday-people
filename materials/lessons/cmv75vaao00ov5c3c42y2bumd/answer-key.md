# Understanding checks and answer key
1. Why is “everyone” a weak audience description?
It does not guide language, practical details or emphasis. A useful example is local adults able to attend with one small household item needing simple repair.

2. What is the difference between understanding and action?
Understanding is what becomes clear: free help is available at the confirmed time/place for one small household item. Action is a supported response: decide whether to attend and prepare one suitable item.

3. Can the draft tell people to register immediately?
No. Registration is not confirmed. Keep the gap explicit and check the method when confirmed. Never invent a booking link or imply registration is unnecessary.

4. What is wrong with a beautiful generic event title?
Decoration does not establish the audience's practical need or response. A focused title/message and supported details should lead.

5. If the audience changes, do source facts change?
No. Emphasis and assumed knowledge can change; verified event facts and unknowns stay fixed. Do not invent volunteer duties or guarantees.

6. Is prepared-comparison.md an AI result?
No. It is a transparent human-authored fixture for comparing focus. Only actual saved tool responses may be described as observed generated output.

7. What if there is no assistant?
Complete the same brief in a regular editor and create a short opening manually. Check it against the source. No subscription is needed.

Accept alternative wording if it preserves audience, useful understanding, supported action, a clear single main message, all consequential facts and visible gaps. Do not score decorative similarity. The five-line template groups verified facts and gaps; current situation and language guidance are supporting fields rather than a conflicting second method.
