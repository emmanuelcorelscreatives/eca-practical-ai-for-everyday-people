# Expected checks and assessment

1. **Permission versus attribution:** permission establishes a valid basis for use; attribution identifies creator/source and satisfies applicable credit conditions. Naming a photographer is not permission to copy.
2. **Source record:** the supplied pattern is original Academy practice artwork under the included CC0 dedication. A generated illustration has no completed record until actually generated and its tool terms reviewed. The unknown online image stays held.
3. **Honest origin:** an actual AI output receives a clear generated-illustration label. An edited image describes relevant edits. A manually composed fallback is labeled manual; do not falsely call it an AI-generated crowd or a photograph.
4. **OCR:** compare actual observed output to `ocr-source-card.txt`. Correct visible names, date and license details. The empty contact field stays empty. No particular OCR error or successful scan is asserted in advance.
5. **Final export:** all three event text fields match the fictional brief; origin label remains readable at viewing size. Inspect image content, not merely metadata.
6. **Handoff:** includes originals, edits, final file, source/permission note and human checklist. Unknowns remain explicit; Publish remains unchecked.

A passing exercise has evidence of checks, not merely boxes pre-ticked. A learner may choose the manual fallback and explain why. No fixed AI output or OCR transcript is required because those observations depend on the actual tool and input.
