# Finish and check
- Can another person identify the decision or explanation, audience and deadline?
- Is the place fictional or real, and are relevant dates explicit?
- Are only factors that could materially affect this purpose included?
- Are tempting unrelated topics explicitly outside scope?
- Are assumptions, generated material, opened evidence and unresolved items separate?
- Have you avoided turning a matching title, citation or polished summary into proof?
- Does the stopping condition support an action while preserving uncertainty?
- Is there a plan to recheck changing facts near the decision?
- If evidence challenges the boundary, is the revision and reason recorded?

Passing the exercise means a clear, honest research boundary—not a fabricated answer or premature event recommendation. Use your own everyday topic next. Do not turn this low-stakes exercise into individualized medical, legal or financial advice.
