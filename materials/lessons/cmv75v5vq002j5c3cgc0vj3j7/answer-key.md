# Expected decisions and final check
These are fictional educational scenarios; laws and organizational requirements still need checking in a real setting.

1. STOP public posting. Private notes permission does not authorize a public clip. Keep the allowed private purpose or seek a fresh specific decision; type a non-identifying summary if its use is appropriate.
2. STOP the portrait input. Visibility is not clearance. Use your own abstract graphic or verify an appropriate licence and relevant people/other rights before reuse.
3. STOP cloning. Recording permission is not synthetic identity permission. Preserve the allowed recording use, record your own voice, or choose an appropriately licensed neutral narrator within its actual conditions.
4. PASS only for the documented private practice scope, after the actual image/audio/captions/access checks. Keep No cloned identity and Public sharing: NOT APPROVED. Do not infer safety from export success alone.
5. PAUSE. Revisit purpose, audience, storage and permission before the changed use; the old private decision cannot approve a public website or longer retention.

## Five supported questions
- Own or cleared? Record actual ownership/licence evidence for each input.
- People approved? Identify every person heard or shown and the relevant permission, or use no identifying person.
- Purpose matches? Compare actual use with the recorded purpose and audience.
- Synthetic use approved? Record specific scope if applicable; this exercise permits no cloning.
- Sharing appropriate? Confirm actual private access and no publication.

All five require support. A blank, assumed or uncertain answer means stop that input and use the manual fallback. Watermarks, metadata, provenance records and detectors are helpful signals; none alone proves truth, consent or authorization. Label substantial edits/synthetic media where identity or authenticity matters; labels do not create missing permission.
