## Harbor challenge key
Split at least the weekday and calendar date as separate assertions (six rows total rather than forcing five categories into five rows). Before opening the pack, name the same kinds of required evidence as above.

- “Friday” and “7 May 2027”: Date; supported by the Harbor notice for the same event and location. Record each separately.
- “It attracted 900 visitors last year”: Quantity; not supported as written. The 2026 register records 900 advance bookings and 760 visitor check-ins. Preserve those definitions; no total from combining categories is established.
- “the city’s favorite book event”: Quotation; misquoted. The original statement is “Among our surveyed volunteers, it was their favorite book event this spring.” Speaker is Amira Solano; volunteer scope and spring period cannot be expanded to the whole city.
- “Families loved it”: Opinion attribution; too broad. Two supplied families express positive views; one reports morning noise. A voluntary sample of three does not establish all families' views.
- “everyone should attend in the morning”: Recommendation; unsupported universal advice. If the goal is the 10:00 storytelling, suggest allowing time to enter before it starts, with entry time unknown. For the quiet-reading goal the schedule offers 14:00; preferences differ. Do not invent queues or a guaranteed entry buffer.
